[paraphrased] Note 33 — Related parties: In accordance with IAS 24, the Bayer Group identifies as related parties (i) entities over which Bayer has joint control or significant influence (joint ventures, joint operations and associated companies accounted for using the equity method), (ii) the Bayer Pension Trust e. V., Leverkusen (BPT), as a related party in its capacity as the contractual trust arrangement (CTA) holding plan assets that fund the German pension and long-term working-time-account obligations of Bayer Group companies, and (iii) the members of the Board of Management and Supervisory Board of Bayer AG, together with their close family members, as key management personnel.

[paraphrased] Transactions with non-consolidated subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures: Sales of goods and rendering of services to, and purchases of goods and services from, equity-method investees and other non-consolidated related entities are concluded in the ordinary course of business and at conditions equivalent to those that apply for arm's-length transactions with unrelated third parties. In 2024 intra-Group services provided to a partner company were offset against services received from the same partner company; the corresponding sales and cost of materials (expenses for purchased services) were therefore not reported separately. The aggregate transaction volumes and outstanding receivables / payables vis-a-vis associates and joint ventures were not material to the Bayer Group's consolidated income statement or statement of financial position.

[verbatim from snippet] "Obligations arising from credit balances on employees' long-term worktime accounts and from pension commitments are either fully or partially secured. The assets invested under individual contractual trust arrangements (CTAs) are offset against the underlying obligations." "Expenses relating to the interest portion of pension and other non-current personnel-related provisions, including the impact of changes in the discount rate, are netted against the income/expense from the assets held by Bayer Pension Trust e. V. (BPT), Germany." "The assets held by BPT serve the sole purpose of meeting pension obligations and the obligations arising from credit balances on employees' long-term worktime accounts, and the Trust's assets are protected from other creditors."

[verbatim from snippet] "The collateral assets are measured at fair value. Their fair value as of December 31, 2024 was EUR 5,613 million (Dec 31, 2023: EUR 5,001 million). Offsetting EUR 1,256 million of the collateral assets against the underlying obligations resulted in a positive difference."

[paraphrased] Note 34 — Total compensation of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board, advances and loans (key management personnel compensation per IAS 24.17):
- Aggregate compensation of the members of the Board of Management for 2024: EUR 25,687 thousand, comprising EUR 9,609 thousand non-performance-related (fixed) components and EUR 5,040 thousand performance-related (short-term variable) components; the remainder relates to long-term variable / share-based components and other benefits.
- Service cost for pension entitlements of active Board of Management members: EUR 13,629 thousand.
- The pension installment for Board members amounts to approximately 30 %–40 % of individual base compensation.
- Compensation of the Supervisory Board and any advances or loans granted to members of the Board of Management or Supervisory Board are disclosed in the Compensation Report 2024; no loans or advances were outstanding to members of the Board of Management or Supervisory Board as of December 31, 2024 [paraphrased].

[paraphrased — Corporate Governance section, p. ~] Pursuant to Sections 111a–111c and 107 of the German Stock Corporation Act (AktG), the Audit Committee of the Supervisory Board approves related-party transactions on behalf of the Supervisory Board and monitors the internal process used to assess whether transactions with related parties are executed in the ordinary course of business and on market terms. No related-party transactions requiring disclosure or public announcement under Section 111c AktG occurred in fiscal year 2024.

## Categories disclosed
- Joint ventures, joint operations and associates accounted for using the equity method (transactions, receivables, payables — disclosed at aggregate level, characterised as not material)
- Bayer Pension Trust e. V., Leverkusen (BPT) — CTA holding plan assets that fund pension and long-term working-time-account obligations
- Key management personnel: Board of Management (fixed, short-term variable, long-term variable, pension service cost) and Supervisory Board (fixed and committee fees)
- Advances and loans to Board of Management / Supervisory Board (nil)
- Related-party transactions subject to AktG Sec. 111a–111c approval (none disclosed for 2024)

## Quantitative highlights (EUR m)
- Sales to RP (associates, JVs, joint operations): not separately quantified in Note 33; characterised as not material; some intra-Group services with a partner company were netted (sales offset against cost of materials) and therefore not reported separately
- Purchases from RP: not separately quantified; see above
- Receivables from RP: not separately quantified; not material
- Payables to RP: not separately quantified; not material
- Loans to / from RP (Board of Management, Supervisory Board): 0 (no advances or loans outstanding)
- KMP compensation — Board of Management aggregate 2024: EUR 25.687 m total (fixed EUR 9.609 m + STI EUR 5.040 m + LTI/share-based + other; pension service cost EUR 13.629 m)
- Bayer Pension Trust e. V. — fair value of collateral assets: EUR 5,613 m (2023: EUR 5,001 m); of which EUR 1,256 m offset against underlying obligations giving rise to a positive difference recognised as a net pension asset

## Arm's length statement
[paraphrased] Transactions between Bayer Group companies and related parties (associates, joint ventures, joint operations and other non-consolidated related entities) are conducted in the ordinary course of business at conditions equivalent to those that apply to arm's-length transactions with unrelated third parties. The Audit Committee of the Supervisory Board monitors the internal process for assessing whether transactions with related parties are executed in the ordinary course of business and on market terms (Sections 111a–111c AktG).
