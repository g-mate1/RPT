## Verbatim disclosure

Hannover Rück SE and its subsidiaries (collectively the "Hannover Re Group" or "Hannover Re") are approximately 50.2% (rounded) owned by Talanx AG, Hannover, and are included in Talanx AG's consolidated financial statements. Talanx AG is in turn majority-owned by HDI Haftpflichtverband der Deutschen Industrie V.a.G. (HDI V.a.G.), Hannover (76.7% as of the relevant reporting date), which is therefore the ultimate parent of Hannover Rück SE. The consolidated financial statements of Talanx AG and HDI V.a.G. are filed with the company register at the Hanover Local Court.

In the ordinary course of its reinsurance business and through Group-wide service and treasury arrangements, the Hannover Re Group enters into transactions with related parties within the meaning of IAS 24 — principally:
(i) Talanx AG and its non-Hannover-Re subsidiaries (HDI Global SE, HDI Global Specialty SE, HDI Versicherung AG, HDI Lebensversicherung AG, Targo Versicherungen, the international HDI primary insurance entities, and HDI Asset Management);
(ii) HDI V.a.G. as ultimate parent;
(iii) non-consolidated subsidiaries and structured entities of the Hannover Re Group itself ("companies of subordinate economic importance" — e.g. Hannover ReTakaful B.S.C. (Manama/Bahrain), FUNIS GmbH & Co. KG (Hannover), Argenta Underwriting Asia Pte. Ltd. (Singapore), Meribel Mottaret Limited (St. Helier/Jersey), Hannover Re Global Alternatives GmbH & Co. KG, HR GLL Central Europe GmbH & Co. KG, Hannover America Private Equity Partners II GmbH & Co. KG, and Hannover Re Euro PE Holdings GmbH & Co. KG);
(iv) associates and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method; and
(v) key management personnel of Hannover Rück SE — the active and former members of the Executive Board and of the Supervisory Board — and their close family members.

Reinsurance business assumed from Talanx-Group primary insurers represents a material related-party flow. Hannover Re Group writes substantial proportional and non-proportional reinsurance cessions from HDI Global SE, HDI Global Specialty SE, HDI Versicherung AG, HDI Lebensversicherung AG and other HDI/Talanx primary carriers; the published Talanx Group risk factors state that "Talanx's primary insurance business purchases a portion of reinsurance protection from its majority-owned subsidiary, the Hannover Re Group". The corresponding written / earned reinsurance premium, paid and incurred claims, technical reserves, ceding commissions and reinsurance receivables / payables are disclosed in the related-party note in the standard IAS 24 categories. [paraphrased]

In addition, the Group maintains service and shared-services agreements with Talanx-Group entities — notably the Talanx Service AG / Talanx Reinsurance Broker GmbH brokerage relationship, asset-management mandates with Ampega Asset Management GmbH / HDI Asset Management, and reciprocal IT, internal-audit and administration services — generating service fees, commissions and intercompany receivables / payables disclosed in the note. [paraphrased]

Key management personnel compensation in accordance with IAS 24.17 is disclosed both in aggregate (total amount EUR 18.0 million in 2024, vs. EUR 15.1 million in the previous year, for active members of the Executive Board and members of the Supervisory Board taken together — being the "compensation of key management personnel of Hannover Re") and broken down into short-term benefits, post-employment benefits, other long-term benefits, termination benefits and share-based payment. During 2024, 31,477 share awards with a fair value of EUR 8.0 million were granted to active members of the Executive Board (Hannover Re share awards, four-year vesting period). Variable compensation of Executive Board members is granted in part in the form of Hannover Re share awards.

The report further states: "The members of the governing bodies did not receive any advances or loans in the year under review, nor were there any other material reportable circumstances or contractual relationships as defined by IAS 24 between companies of the Hannover Re Group and the members of the governing bodies or their related parties."
