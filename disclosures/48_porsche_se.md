**Related parties (extract) — [paraphrased]**

[paraphrased] Related parties as defined by IAS 24 are natural persons and entities on which the Porsche SE Group can exert significant influence, or which can exert significant influence on the Porsche SE Group, including subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates of the Porsche SE Group, as well as those of the Volkswagen Group. Related parties also comprise persons and entities that can be influenced by the Porsche and Piëch families (the ultimate controlling shareholders), in particular the Familie Porsche AG Beteiligungsgesellschaft, Ferdinand Porsche Familien-Holding GmbH, Porsche Wolfgang 1. Beteiligungsverwaltungs GmbH, Porsche Holding Gesellschaft m.b.H. (Porsche Holding Salzburg) and their affiliates, as well as members of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board of Porsche SE and their close family members.

[paraphrased] As of 31 December 2024, Porsche Automobil Holding SE held 53.3% of the voting rights in Volkswagen AG, Wolfsburg, and is therefore the ultimate parent of the Volkswagen Group. In addition, Porsche SE holds 25% plus one share of the ordinary shares of Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, Stuttgart. The State of Lower Saxony has rights of appointment under the VW Act and, as long as it holds at least 15% of Volkswagen AG's ordinary shares, Porsche SE cannot alone elect all shareholder representatives to the Supervisory Board of Volkswagen AG. Porsche SE is nevertheless classified as a related party of the Volkswagen Group under IAS 24.

[paraphrased] Following the IPO of Porsche AG and the sale of ordinary shares to Porsche SE in fiscal year 2022, Porsche SE and Volkswagen AG entered into a "procedural and amendment agreement and agreement to amend the Comprehensive Agreement", which led to amendments to certain provisions regarding the appointment to governing bodies of Porsche AG contained in the Comprehensive Agreement. The Comprehensive Agreement continues to govern the cooperation between Porsche SE and Volkswagen AG.

[paraphrased] In fiscal year 2024, Porsche SE received dividend distributions of approximately €1.4 billion from Volkswagen AG and approximately €0.3 billion from Porsche AG, in total **€1,703 million** (previous year: €1,529 million). These dividends are not included in the related-party tables.

[paraphrased] All other transactions with related parties are carried out at arm's length on the basis of internationally accepted methods in accordance with IAS 24. Group-internal balances and transactions with consolidated subsidiaries of Porsche SE are eliminated on consolidation and are not disclosed as related-party transactions.

[paraphrased] Transactions with associates accounted for using the equity method (in particular Volkswagen AG and Porsche AG and their subsidiaries) and with other related parties (in particular companies controlled by the Porsche and Piëch families, e.g. Porsche Holding Salzburg) primarily relate to services, consulting and ancillary cost reimbursements, as well as financing relationships (cash investments, short-term loans).
