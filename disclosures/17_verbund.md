Under IAS 24, Verbund identifies the following parties as related to the Verbund Group: (i) the **Republic of Austria**, which holds 51.0% of the share capital of Verbund AG directly (administered through ÖBAG — Österreichische Beteiligungs AG) and is therefore the ultimate controlling party; (ii) the **syndicate partners** with which the Republic of Austria has formed a voting syndicate, namely the **state-utility (Landesenergiegesellschaft) "EVN-Wien Energie syndicate"** (Wiener Stadtwerke GmbH / EVN AG holding together c.25% of voting rights), classified as parties with significant influence over Verbund; (iii) all entities controlled, jointly controlled or significantly influenced by the Republic of Austria — in particular other Austrian federal-government-related entities such as **ÖBB-Infrastruktur AG / ÖBB-Holding AG** (Austrian Federal Railways group), **ASFINAG**, **Österreichische Bundesforste AG**, **Österreichische Post AG**, **Telekom Austria AG**, **OMV AG** (also state-controlled via ÖBAG) and other ÖBAG-portfolio companies — for which Verbund applies the exemption for "government-related entities" under IAS 24.25-27, disclosing only individually or collectively significant transactions; (iv) the Group's **associates and joint ventures** accounted for using the equity method, including but not limited to Energie Klagenfurt GmbH, Ennskraftwerke AG, KELAG-Kärntner Elektrizitäts-Aktiengesellschaft, Donaukraftwerk Jochenstein AG, Österreichisch-Bayerische Kraftwerke AG, Kraftwerk Nußdorf Errichtungs- und BetriebsgmbH, and other joint hydro/wind project companies; (v) **non-consolidated subsidiaries**; and (vi) **key management personnel (KMP)** of Verbund AG — the Executive Board (Vorstand) and Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) — together with their close family members and entities controlled by them. [paraphrased]

Transactions with related parties are carried out **in the ordinary course of business and on arm's-length terms** (zu marktüblichen Konditionen). Categories of transactions disclosed include: (a) sales of electricity, gas, ancillary services, grid services and other goods/services to associates, joint ventures and government-related entities (notably significant electricity supply and grid-balancing transactions with ÖBB-Infrastruktur AG, electricity sales to and purchases from state-related and provincial-utility (E-Werke / Landesenergieversorger) related parties); (b) purchases of electricity, fuels, grid services and other goods/services from associates, joint ventures and government-related entities; (c) outstanding trade receivables and payables vis-à-vis related parties at the balance-sheet date; (d) financing/loan relationships, including loans extended to or received from associates and joint ventures (e.g. partner-loan financing of joint hydro plants), and including financing arrangements with the federally-owned Oesterreichische Kontrollbank (OeKB) (export-finance and KMU-financing facilities); (e) dividends received from equity-accounted investees; (f) dividends paid to the Republic of Austria and other shareholders (a substantial cash outflow given Verbund's c.55% pay-out policy); (g) compensation of key management personnel split into short-term benefits, post-employment benefits (pension contributions), other long-term benefits, termination benefits and share/LTI-based payments; and (h) attendance fees and other remuneration of the Supervisory Board. [paraphrased]

The disclosure also notes that Verbund avails itself of the **government-related-entity exemption** under IAS 24.25 with respect to individually insignificant transactions with other entities ultimately controlled by the Republic of Austria, but provides qualitative and, where individually or collectively material, quantitative information on the nature and extent of such transactions. ## Categories disclosed
- Ultimate controlling party: **Republic of Austria (51.0%)** — administered via ÖBAG
- Significant-influence shareholders / **syndicate partners**: Wiener Stadtwerke GmbH and EVN AG (jointly c.25% of voting rights via syndicate agreement)
- Other **government-related entities** under common control of the Republic of Austria (IAS 24.25 exemption applied): ÖBB group, ASFINAG, Bundesforste, Österreichische Post, Telekom Austria, OMV, OeKB, etc.
- Transactions with **associates and joint ventures** accounted for using the equity method (hydro/wind partner-companies, KELAG, Ennskraftwerke, Energie Klagenfurt, Donaukraftwerk Jochenstein, Österreichisch-Bayerische Kraftwerke, etc.)
- **Non-consolidated subsidiaries**
- Sales of electricity, grid services, gas and other goods/services to related parties
- Purchases of electricity, fuels and services from related parties
- **Receivables** from related parties at year-end
- **Payables** to related parties at year-end
- **Loans to / loans from** related parties (partner-loan financing of joint power plants; OeKB financing facilities)
- **Dividends received** from equity-accounted investees
- **Dividends paid** to the Republic of Austria and other shareholders
- **Key management personnel (KMP) compensation** — split into short-term, post-employment, other long-term, termination, and share-based/LTI components
- **Supervisory Board remuneration** (attendance fees)
- Pension-related transactions (post-employment benefits accruing to KMP and former Board members)
- Arm's-length statement
