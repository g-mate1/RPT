[paraphrased] **Related parties.** In addition to its consolidated subsidiaries, Infineon Technologies AG is in a related party relationship with non-consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures, associated companies, and the members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board of Infineon Technologies AG (key management personnel) and their close family members. Joint ventures and associated companies of the Infineon group, which are accounted for using the equity method, include in particular European Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (ESMC) GmbH, Dresden — the joint venture established in 2023/24 with TSMC (70%), Bosch (10%), Infineon (10%) and NXP (10%) for the construction and operation of a 300 mm semiconductor fab in Dresden — to which Infineon has committed an equity contribution in the order of EUR 500 million over the build-out phase.

[paraphrased] **Transactions with joint ventures and associated companies.** Business transactions arising from ordinary operations with non-consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associated companies (purchases of goods/services, sales, recharges of shared services, and outstanding balances) were not material to the consolidated financial statements of the Infineon group as at and for the year ended 30 September 2024. The share of profit from associates and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method recognised in the consolidated income statement was EUR 11 million in FY2024.

[paraphrased] **Compensation of key management personnel (Management Board and Supervisory Board).** Total compensation of the Management Board members of Infineon Technologies AG for the 2024 fiscal year is set out in the Remuneration Report 2024, which forms an integral part of the annual disclosures and to which the Related Parties note expressly refers. Within the maximum-compensation framework adopted by the Annual General Meeting (cap of EUR 8.2 million for the CEO and EUR 5.9 million for each ordinary Management Board member), the target total remuneration for FY2024 was EUR 4.0 million for the CEO (Jochen Hanebeck) and EUR 2.3 million for each ordinary Management Board member, comprising fixed base salary, fringe benefits, company pension service cost, short-term variable compensation (PSP/STI) and long-term share-based compensation (PSP/LTI). Supervisory Board members receive fixed remuneration plus committee fees pursuant to Section 14 of the Articles of Association.

[paraphrased] **Approval procedure.** Verbatim from the Corporate Governance Statement 2024 (which is incorporated by reference into the Related Parties / Corporate Governance disclosures): *"There were no related party transactions requiring approval in the 2024 fiscal year. Publicly listed companies such as Infineon require the approval of the Supervisory Board or one of its committees before entering into certain transactions with related parties. In order to identify related party transactions that require approval and to treat them in accordance with the law, Infineon has implemented a procedure based on a global Group guideline. The Supervisory Board has delegated responsibility in this area to its Investment, Finance and Audit Committee, particularly for resolutions requiring approval."*

## Arm's length statement
[paraphrased] Transactions with related parties are entered into on an arm's-length basis (at conditions comparable to those applied with unrelated third parties), and where they meet the approval thresholds under Sec. 111b AktG they are subject to approval by the Supervisory Board's Investment, Finance and Audit Committee in accordance with Infineon's global Group guideline. No related party transactions requiring approval occurred in the 2024 fiscal year.
