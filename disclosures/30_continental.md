Related parties of the Continental Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise (i) non-consolidated subsidiaries, (ii) associated companies and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method, (iii) the IHO Group, in particular INA-Holding Schaeffler GmbH & Co. KG, Herzogenaurach, Germany ("INA-Holding Schaeffler") — which is the ultimate parent of the IHO Group and the indirect controlling shareholder of Continental AG — together with its subsidiaries and sister companies (notably the Schaeffler group), (iv) the Schaeffler family as the controlling shareholders of INA-Holding Schaeffler, and (v) key management personnel of Continental AG (members of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board) and their close family members. [paraphrased]

INA-Holding Schaeffler GmbH & Co. KG prepares consolidated financial statements that include Continental AG. In connection with the German Minimum Tax Act (Mindeststeuergesetz, MinStG) implementing the OECD Pillar Two rules, the minimum tax is levied at the level of INA-Holding Schaeffler as the ultimate parent entity of the IHO Group. Continental AG is treated as a partially-owned parent entity that is jointly assessed with the other companies of the IHO Group. Continental AG is obligated under Section 3 (6) MinStG to compensate INA-Holding Schaeffler for the share of the minimum tax attributable to Continental AG. [paraphrased]

Transactions with related parties are conducted on arm's length terms. [paraphrased]

Transactions with associated companies and joint ventures principally consist of sales of goods and services, purchases of goods and services, as well as the related trade receivables and trade payables. [...] [paraphrased]

Transactions with the Schaeffler group and other IHO-Group companies principally consist of the sale and purchase of goods and services in the ordinary course of business between sister companies; corresponding trade receivables and trade payables are recognised. [paraphrased]

Total compensation of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board (key management personnel under IAS 24) is disclosed in the remuneration report and comprises short-term employee benefits (fixed remuneration, fringe benefits, short-term variable compensation), post-employment benefits (pension service cost), other long-term benefits (long-term variable compensation / share-based components) and termination benefits where applicable. [paraphrased]
