Related parties of the Mayr-Melnhof Karton AG Group comprise:
- joint ventures of the Group (companies accounted for using the equity method);
- key management personnel of the Group, namely the active members of the Management Board and the Supervisory Board of Mayr-Melnhof Karton AG, together with their close relatives; and
- other related companies, including companies of the core shareholder families of Mayr-Melnhof Karton AG (Mayr-Melnhof family-related foundations and holding entities, including the MM Salzburg Privatstiftung and the MMS Mayr-Melnhof-Saurau Beteiligungsverwaltung KG and affiliated foundations) and their controlled or significantly influenced entities.

[paraphrased] Transactions with joint ventures during the year mainly comprised purchases of raw materials (used for cartonboard production) and the sale of intermediate products and services. Trade liabilities to joint ventures amounted to EUR 608 thousand as at 30 June 2024 (half-year figure disclosed in HY 2024 report; the FY 2024 closing balance is disclosed in the annual report's related-party note); raw materials purchased from joint ventures amounted to EUR 2,648 thousand in H1 2024 (H1 2023: EUR 3,243 thousand). The full-year FY2024 figures continue this pattern but specific FY-end totals could not be retrieved verbatim.

[paraphrased] Transactions with other related companies — primarily purchases of raw materials for cartonboard production from family-related entities — were carried out during the year. In H1 2024 such purchases amounted to EUR 4,737 thousand.

[paraphrased] Expenses for consulting services rendered to the Group by a member of the Supervisory Board amounted to EUR 10 thousand in H1 2024 (H1 2023: EUR 4 thousand). Standard market rates were charged for these consulting services.

[paraphrased] Total compensation of key management personnel (active Management Board members) is disclosed in the consolidated remuneration/personnel note. Supervisory Board compensation for the 2024 financial year totalled approximately EUR 740,014 (per FY2024 Remuneration Report and Supervisory Board report).

[paraphrased] Further information on the composition and remuneration of the Management Board and Supervisory Board is provided in the Consolidated Corporate Governance Report and the Remuneration Report, which form part of the FY2024 annual reporting package.
