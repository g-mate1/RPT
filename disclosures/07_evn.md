Under IAS 24, EVN identifies its related parties as: (i) the Province of Lower Austria (Land Niederösterreich), which via NÖ Landes-Beteiligungsholding GmbH, St. Pölten holds 51.0% of EVN AG's share capital (a statutorily mandated minimum stake), making it the ultimate controlling party; (ii) Wiener Stadtwerke GmbH, Vienna (28.4%), classified as a related party able to exercise significant influence and party to a syndicate agreement; (iii) entities controlled or jointly controlled by, or under significant influence of, the Province of Lower Austria; (iv) associates and joint ventures of the EVN Group accounted for using the equity method — principally Verbund Innkraftwerke GmbH, the EnergieAllianz Austria (EAA) group (joint sales/trading vehicle held with Wien Energie and Energie Burgenland), RAG Austria AG, and Burgenland Holding AG; and (v) members of the Executive Board (Vorstand) and Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) of EVN AG (key management personnel, KMP), together with their close family members. [paraphrased]

Transactions with these related parties are conducted in the ordinary course of business and at arm's-length conditions (zu marktüblichen Bedingungen). Material categories disclosed include: electricity, gas and heat supplies to and purchases from EAA group joint ventures and from associates within the Austrian electricity sector; network usage charges; service-level/shared-service charges; deposits and short-term financing relationships; and dividends received from associates (notably the dividend stream from Verbund-related investments). EVN also discloses outstanding receivables and payables vis-à-vis associates/joint ventures at the balance sheet date, with no material impairments recognised on related-party receivables. [paraphrased]

Key management personnel compensation is disclosed in aggregate (short-term, post-employment, other long-term and termination benefits) in the remuneration report and cross-referenced from the IAS 24 note. Remuneration of the Supervisory Board (attendance fees) is also disclosed. [paraphrased]
