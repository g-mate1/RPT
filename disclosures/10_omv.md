Identification of related parties. Enterprises and individuals are considered to be related if one party is able to control or exercise significant influence over the business or financial decisions of the other. OMV Aktiengesellschaft's major shareholders are Österreichische Beteiligungs AG ("ÖBAG"), which holds 31.5%, and Abu Dhabi National Oil Company P.J.S.C. ("ADNOC"), which holds 24.9%. Both are related parties under IAS 24. The 24.9% ADNOC stake was transferred from Mubadala Investment Company to ADNOC on February 28, 2024. The Republic of Austria has an indirect relationship with OMV via ÖBAG and is therefore, together with companies under the control of the Republic of Austria, considered a related party. Related parties further comprise equity-accounted investments (joint ventures and associates), key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board), and entities controlled or significantly influenced by those persons.

Transactions with Austrian-state-controlled entities. In its normal course of business, OMV has arm's length transactions mainly with Österreichische Post Aktiengesellschaft, VERBUND AG, Österreichische Bundesbahnen-Holding Aktiengesellschaft, Bundesbeschaffung GmbH and their subsidiaries. In 2024, OMV concluded a power purchase agreement with VERBUND for the supply of sustainable electricity from hydropower for four years starting in January 2025.

Transactions with ADNOC/Abu Dhabi-related entities. In 2024, there were supplies of goods and services to Abu Dhabi Company for Offshore Petroleum Operations Ltd, NOVA Chemicals Corporation (NOVA), and Abu Dhabi National Oil Company P.J.S.C. OMV cooperates with ADNOC in several Exploration & Production arrangements and has closed strategic equity partnerships with ADNOC covering both the ADNOC Refining business and a Trading joint venture.

Transactions with equity-accounted investments. In 2024 and 2023, there were arm's length supplies of goods and services (including the granting of licenses for the use of technologies belonging to the Group) between the Group and equity-accounted companies. OMV's principal equity-accounted investees include Borouge plc, Borouge 4 LLC, Bayport Polymers LLC, Abu Dhabi Petroleum Investments LLC (ADPINV), Pak-Arab Refinery Limited (PARCO), Pearl Petroleum Company Limited, Abu Dhabi Oil Refining Company (15%), and Erdöl-Lagergesellschaft m.b.H.

Loans and financial commitments to related parties.
- Member loan to Bayport Polymers LLC (joint venture): EUR 769 mn including accrued interest as of December 31, 2024 (December 31, 2023: EUR 701 mn).
- Shareholder loan to Borouge 4 LLC (joint venture): EUR 435 mn drawn plus accrued interest as of December 31, 2024 (December 31, 2023: EUR 155 mn). Undrawn portion of the EUR 1,028 mn commitment: EUR 615 mn as of December 31, 2024 (December 31, 2023: EUR 818 mn). EUR 413 mn out of the total EUR 1,028 mn commitment had been drawn as of December 31, 2024. The shareholder loan agreement was entered into on February 3, 2023.
- Loan receivable from Electrocentrale Borzești S.R.L. (held via OMV Petrom): EUR 25 mn as of December 31, 2024.
- Credit facility of EUR 193 mn plus interest as of December 31, 2024 (50% guaranteed by Borealis, remaining 50% guaranteed by joint-venture partner TotalEnergies).

Guarantees granted to equity-accounted investments.
- Borealis AG parental guarantee for senior notes of Bayport Polymers LLC: EUR 626 mn plus interest as of December 31, 2024 (December 31, 2023: EUR 588 mn).
- Borealis AG guarantee for funding of Borouge 4 LLC under the Italian Export Credit Agency agreement: total guarantee EUR 1,228 mn plus interest; guaranteed amount based on drawn financing was EUR 1,009 mn as of December 31, 2024.
- Income from financial guarantees granted to equity-accounted investments: EUR 7 mn in 2024 (2023: EUR 5 mn).

Other related party items.
- Non-monetary transactions with Erdöl-Lagergesellschaft m.b.H. outside the scope of IFRS 15 (not included in "Sales and other income"): EUR 5 mn in 2024 (2023: EUR 51 mn). The contract liabilities toward Erdöl-Lagergesellschaft m.b.H. relate to a long-term contract for rendering of services.

Compensation of key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board). Supervisory Board remuneration expense in 2024 amounted to EUR 1.1 mn. Detailed breakdown of Executive Board compensation (short-term benefits, post-employment benefits, share-based payments, termination benefits) is presented in the separate Remuneration Report for the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board 2024 and in the Notes. Defined benefit-related contributions for 2024 to APK-Pensionskasse AG amounted to EUR 5,568 thousand. Pension fund contribution for Executive Board members equals 25% of base salary, paid to APK Pensionskasse AG. Members of the Executive Board and the members of the Supervisory Board are covered by directors and officers' liability insurance (D&O) and criminal legal expenses insurance (lump-sum premiums not specifically attributed to Board members).
