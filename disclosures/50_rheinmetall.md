**[paraphrased]** Related parties of the Rheinmetall Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise:
- non-consolidated subsidiaries,
- associates and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method (32 companies in FY2024; previous year: 35) — including, among others, ARGE Puma (project consortium with KNDS for the German Army Puma IFV), Rheinmetall Italia/LRMV (50/50 JV with Leonardo S.p.A., established October 2024), Rheinmetall Norway-related entities, Pierburg-related JVs in China, and the Ukrainian Defense Industry JV (RDU) in Kyiv,
- 9 joint operations (previous year: 8),
- key management personnel (the members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of Rheinmetall AG), and
- companies controlled or significantly influenced by such key management personnel.

**[paraphrased]** Transactions with related parties primarily consist of sales of goods and services to, and purchases of goods and services from, equity-accounted joint ventures and associates in the ordinary course of business (notably project deliveries to ARGE Puma, technology and component supplies to/from automotive joint ventures, and deliveries to the Leonardo Rheinmetall Military Vehicles JV from October 2024). Outstanding receivables and payables vis-à-vis these entities are reported within trade receivables / trade payables and other financial assets / liabilities, respectively. There were no material loans to or from related parties at the reporting date other than ordinary intercompany financing with non-consolidated subsidiaries. No guarantees of a material nature were granted to related parties beyond those disclosed under contingent liabilities.

**[paraphrased]** Remuneration of key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board of Rheinmetall AG): Total remuneration of the Executive Board for FY2024 increased significantly versus FY2023 driven by the 12% increase in basic remuneration and STI target for Mr Papperger, Ms Steinert and Mr Krause approved by the Supervisory Board, plus higher STI achievement and LTI fair values reflecting record FY2024 results. Total Executive Board remuneration granted/owed for FY2024 was in the order of EUR 12–15 million (aggregate, all members; exact figure per the Remuneration Report 2024). Supervisory Board remuneration was approximately EUR 2 million in aggregate. Pension obligations (DBO) toward current and former Executive Board members and surviving dependents are reported within pension provisions in the consolidated balance sheet.

**[paraphrased — arm's length statement]** All transactions with related parties are conducted on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's length transactions (i.e., on standard market conditions).
