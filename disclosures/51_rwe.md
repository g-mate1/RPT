Related parties of the RWE Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise non-consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates of the RWE Group as well as the members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of RWE AG (key management personnel) and their close family members. Key shareholders of RWE AG include institutional investors and a number of municipal utility shareholders (notably KEB Holding AG and RW Energie-Beteiligungsgesellschaft mbH & Co. KG) which together hold a notable minority stake. RWE itself holds a 15.0% stake in E.ON SE (acquired through the 2019 asset swap) which is accounted for as an investment at fair value; E.ON SE is therefore not classified as a related party of RWE in the IAS 24 sense.

Significant related-party transactions arose primarily with associates and joint ventures of the Group, in particular Amprion GmbH (25.1%), URANIT GmbH (50%), and various offshore-wind project companies jointly held with TotalEnergies, Masdar and other partners. Transactions consist mainly of the sale and purchase of electricity, gas, grid services, project-development services and the provision of intra-group financing.

All transactions with related parties were carried out at arm's length, i.e. on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's length transactions between independent parties.

The total remuneration of the members of the Executive Board of RWE AG for fiscal 2024 amounted to approximately EUR 11.756 million (current Executive Board members, including share-based and long-term components recognised in the period). The aggregate remuneration of the Supervisory Board for fiscal 2024 amounted to approximately EUR 3.480 million. Pension obligations and additions to pension provisions for former Executive Board members are disclosed in the Remuneration Report. [...]
