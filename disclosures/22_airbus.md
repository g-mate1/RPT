**Related Parties.** The Company's related parties consist of (i) its principal shareholders — Société de Gestion de Participations Aéronautiques ("Sogepa", France), Gesellschaft zur Beteiligungsverwaltung GZBV mbH & Co. KG ("GZBV", Germany, controlled by KfW) and Sociedad Estatal de Participaciones Industriales ("SEPI", Spain) — which are holding companies for the French, German and Spanish States respectively and which together held approximately 25.7% of Airbus's share capital at 31 December 2024 (Sogepa 10.8%, GZBV 10.8%, SEPI 4.1%); (ii) its joint ventures, principally ArianeGroup Holding S.A.S. (50%), MBDA S.A.S. (37.5%) and ATR GIE (50%); (iii) its associates accounted for under the equity method; (iv) the Company's pension plans/funds; and (v) Members of the Board of Directors and the Executive Committee (key management personnel).

Transactions with the French, German and Spanish States and entities controlled by them are conducted in the ordinary course of business and primarily relate to defence and government-customer programmes (e.g. military aircraft, helicopters, space, MBDA missiles). Such transactions are carried out on an arm's length basis. Information on transactions with the Company's joint ventures (sales of goods and services to, and purchases from, ArianeGroup, MBDA and ATR, together with related receivables, payables and commitments) is disclosed in the Joint Ventures and Associates note. Transactions with unconsolidated subsidiaries are immaterial to the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements.

As of 31 December 2024, the Company had granted guarantees of €104 million to NHI Industries in France (2023: €104 million).

Information regarding the funding of the Company's pension plans (which are considered as related parties) is provided in Note "Post-Employment Benefits" (Note 31).

Information relating to compensation and benefits granted to Members of the Executive Committee and the Board of Directors of Airbus SE (key management personnel) is provided in the Remuneration Report contained in the Report of the Board of Directors 2024. The total compensation of the Members of the Executive Committee comprises short-term employee benefits (base salary and annual variable remuneration), post-employment benefits (pension service cost), share-based payments (long-term incentive / Performance Unit Plan) and social charges. The CEO's annual variable pay for FY2024 decreased by 13% to €1.69 million.
