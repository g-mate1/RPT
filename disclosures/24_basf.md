## Verbatim disclosure
"Related parties are legal or natural entities that can exert influence on the BASF Group or over which the BASF Group exercises control or joint control, or a significant influence. These primarily include nonconsolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associated companies as well as BASF SE's Board of Executive Directors and Supervisory Board."

"Transactions with related parties are carried out under normal market conditions."

"The tables show the volume of business with related parties that are included in the Consolidated Financial Statements at amortized cost or accounted for using the equity method. Sales and trade accounts receivable from and trade accounts payable to related parties mainly included business with own products and merchandise, agency and licensing businesses, and other operating businesses." ## Categories disclosed
- Nonconsolidated subsidiaries (group companies not fully consolidated)
- Joint ventures (e.g., BASF-YPC Co. Ltd. / Verbund Site Nanjing; long-term power purchase agreement counterparties)
- Associated companies (equity-method investees)
- BASF Pensionskasse VVaG (post-employment benefit plan entity / pension fund) — rentals, financial receivables and financial liabilities
- Key management personnel: Board of Executive Directors and Supervisory Board (cross-reference to Note 29 for KMP compensation)
- Note: Wintershall Dea was no longer a BASF associate in 2024 (BASF completed the sale of its Wintershall Dea upstream E&P business to Harbour Energy on September 3, 2024; Wintershall Dea was therefore deconsolidated/derecognized as an equity-method investment during 2024 — disclosed in the Acquisitions & Divestitures note rather than as a recurring RPT counterparty for year-end 2024)
