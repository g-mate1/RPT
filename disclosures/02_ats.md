[paraphrased] Related parties of AT&S Austria Technologie & Systemtechnik Aktiengesellschaft consist of: (a) the shareholders with notifiable holdings — Androsch Privatstiftung (ca. 15.42 % of share capital), Dörflinger Privatstiftung (ca. 12.09 %), and Dörflinger Management & Beteiligungen GmbH (ca. 5.94 %), and persons exercising joint control through these vehicles; (b) non-consolidated subsidiaries and joint ventures of the AT&S Group; (c) members of the Management Board (Vorstand) and of the Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) of AT&S AG and their close family members; and (d) companies controlled or significantly influenced by those persons or entities, including the law firm Frotz Riedl Rechtsanwälte (Vienna), to which the Chairman of the Supervisory Board from 18 December 2024, Dr. Georg Riedl, is a partner. Dr. Hannes Androsch served as Chairman of the Supervisory Board until his death on 11 December 2024; Dr. Georg Riedl succeeded him on 18 December 2024 and continued in office through the end of the fiscal year on 31 March 2025. At the AGM on 3 July 2025 (after the balance-sheet date), Andy Mattes was elected Chairman of the Supervisory Board and Dr. Georg Riedl became Deputy Chairman.

[paraphrased] Transactions with related parties were carried out on customary market terms (arm's-length basis). They comprise (i) legal advisory and consulting services received from law firms in which members of the Supervisory Board hold an interest (in particular Frotz Riedl Rechtsanwälte), and (ii) the ordinary remuneration of members of the Management Board and the Supervisory Board. There were no material loans to or from related parties, no guarantees granted to or received from related parties, no material transactions with the Androsch or Dörflinger private foundations beyond their shareholder position (no dividend was paid in respect of FY2024/25, as resolved by the AGM on 3 July 2025), and no real-estate transactions with related parties. Transactions with non-consolidated subsidiaries and with equity-accounted investees were not material to the Group's financial position or results.

[paraphrased] Key Management Personnel (KMP) compensation — FY2024/25 (Management Board):
- Pension-fund contributions for the active Management Board members for FY2024/25 amounted to EUR 44,000 each for Petra Preining (CFO), Peter Griehsnig (CTO), Peter Schneider (interim CEO from late 2024) and Ingolf Schröder (COO).
- For the former CEO Andreas Gerstenmayer (who stepped down during FY2024/25 following an internal disagreement), pension-fund contributions for the period amounted to EUR 140,000 (prior year: EUR 64,000).
- Each of Preining, Griehsnig, Schneider and Schröder received a one-time special bonus of EUR 95,000 for the fiscal year, in recognition of the exceptional burden and additional duties carried by the board during the period.
- Prior-year disclosure for Andreas Gerstenmayer included share-price-appreciation-right (LTI) compensation valued at EUR 519,000.

[paraphrased] Supervisory Board remuneration — FY2024/25: The fixed and committee-related attendance fees for the members of the Supervisory Board are disclosed individually in the Remuneration Report 2024/25 (approved by the AGM on 3 July 2025).
