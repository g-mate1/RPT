## Verbatim disclosure

**[paraphrased]** The related party disclosures note (IAS 24) of MTU Aero Engines AG's FY2024 consolidated financial statements covers:

(a) **Related parties of the Group** — defined as (i) non-consolidated subsidiaries, (ii) joint ventures accounted for using the equity method (principally MTU Maintenance Zhuhai Co., Ltd. (China; 50% MTU / 50% China Southern Airlines), EME Aero Sp. z o.o. (Poland; 50/50 with Lufthansa Technik), and the newly established EURA joint venture (50/50 with Safran Helicopter Engines, set up June 2024) plus EUMET S.A.S. (50/50 with Safran Aircraft Engines for the FCAS / NGF program)), (iii) associates accounted for using the equity method (including Airfoil Services Sdn. Bhd. (Malaysia)), (iv) the members of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board of MTU Aero Engines AG and their close family members (key management personnel), and (v) the post-employment defined benefit plans for the Group's employees (the "MTU pension trust / CTA").

(b) **Transactions with joint ventures and associates** — In the ordinary course of business, MTU enters into transactions with its joint ventures and associates, principally consisting of (i) sales of new and used spare parts, engine modules and components to the joint venture MRO shops (notably MTU Maintenance Zhuhai and EME Aero) for use in third-party engine overhauls, (ii) purchases of MRO services and certain components from those joint ventures, (iii) licensing of MTU intellectual property and engineering services to the JV companies, and (iv) treasury services including short-term funding lines. Receivables and payables arise from these trading activities and are unsecured, settled in cash, and bear no interest in the short term.

(c) **Transactions with shareholders / industrial program partners** — MTU notes that program partnerships under risk-and-revenue-sharing arrangements (RRSP) — notably with Pratt & Whitney (RTX) on the V2500, PW1000G GTF family, PW6000, PW2000; with GE Aerospace on the GE9X, GEnx, CF6, GP7000; and with Rolls-Royce on the Trent family — are **not** treated as IAS 24 related party transactions because none of those partners has the ability to exercise significant influence or control over MTU. They are arm's-length industrial cooperations.

(d) **Key management personnel (KMP) compensation** — Total compensation of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board for FY2024 is disclosed, split into short-term benefits (fixed salary, short-term variable / annual bonus, fringe benefits), post-employment benefits (service cost for pension entitlements), long-term variable / share-based payments (Performance Share Plan), and termination benefits. Detailed individualised disclosures are presented in the Management Compensation Report 2024.

(e) **Pension obligations / CTA** — MTU's contractual trust arrangement (the funding vehicle for German pension obligations) is a related party. Contributions and reimbursements during the year are disclosed.

(f) **Arm's-length statement** — **[paraphrased]** MTU states that all transactions with related parties were conducted under conditions equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions.

(g) **Section 111a–c AktG statement (German Stock Corporation Act)** — Consistent with its 2023 disclosure, MTU's Supervisory Board confirmed that it adopted an internal procedure in 2020 to comply with the legal requirements for related party transactions of stock corporations; **in the FY2024 reporting period, no transactions requiring Supervisory Board consent or ad-hoc disclosure under Sec. 111a–c AktG occurred**.

## Arm's length statement
**[paraphrased]** MTU states that transactions with related parties are concluded on terms equivalent to those prevailing in arm's-length transactions.
