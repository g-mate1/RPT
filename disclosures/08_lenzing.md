**Scope of related parties.** Related parties of the Lenzing Group comprise the member companies of the B&C Group together with its subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates, the corporate bodies (Managing Board and Supervisory Board) of Lenzing AG, as well as close family members of members of corporate bodies and companies under their influence. Investments in associates and joint ventures are accounted for using the equity method.

**Shareholder structure changes (2024).** On June 12, 2024, Suzano S.A. acquired a 15 percent interest in Lenzing AG from the B&C Group subject to a condition precedent; the transaction closed on August 30, 2024. As of December 31, 2024 the B&C Group held 37.25 percent of the voting rights and Suzano S.A. held 15 percent of the voting rights in Lenzing AG.

**B&C Group — tax-group withdrawal and tax allocations.** The termination agreement negotiated on the occasion of the withdrawal of Lenzing AG from the tax group of B&C Holding Österreich GmbH was signed on September 10, 2024. Lenzing AG paid a tax allocation of EUR 22,209 thousand plus interest of EUR 1,280 thousand to B&C Holding Österreich GmbH for losses of the foreign group members of the tax group of Lenzing AG that had not yet been taxed. In addition, a tax allocation of EUR 810 thousand plus interest of EUR 65 thousand was paid to B&C Holding Österreich GmbH for the 2021 financial year following the completion of an external tax audit.

**Bickel Austria GmbH (KMP-related entity).** Walter Bickel, Managing Director of Bickel Austria GmbH, Vienna, has been a member of the Managing Board of Lenzing AG since April 15, 2024. In the 2024 financial year, an amount of EUR 4,399 thousand was paid to Bickel Austria GmbH for consulting services rendered. No liabilities are outstanding as at December 31, 2024.

**Transactions with companies accounted for using the equity method.**
- LD Florestal S.A. received a long-term, unsecured loan from the fully consolidated subsidiary LD Celulose S.A.; the outstanding amount was EUR 29,871 thousand as of December 31, 2024 (December 31, 2023: EUR 13,679 thousand). The interest reflects standard bank rates and interest income of EUR 2,500 thousand was recognised in 2024.
- Kelheim Fibers GmbH, a subsidiary of the equity-accounted investee EQUI-Fibres Beteiligungsgesellschaft mbH, received a long-term, unsecured loan of EUR 5,000 thousand from Lenzing AG in 2017; this loan, including unpaid interest, has been written down in its entirety.
- An expense of EUR 939 thousand was recognised in 2024 from impairment losses on trade receivables due from companies accounted for at equity.

**Key management personnel compensation.** The remuneration expensed for key management personnel — comprising the active members of the Managing Board and Supervisory Board of Lenzing AG — is disclosed in the IAS 24 categories (short-term benefits, post-employment benefits, other long-term benefits, termination benefits, share-based payments) in the dedicated table within the note; readers are referred to the separate Remuneration Report of the Lenzing Group 2024 and to the Governance chapter "Remuneration of the Managing Board and the Supervisory Board" for the detailed breakdown. The EUR 4,399 thousand paid to Bickel Austria GmbH (above) is reported in addition to the standard Managing Board remuneration.

**Other.** Lenzing's joint venture RVL Reststoffverwertung Lenzing GmbH (with Energie AG Umwelt Service) continued under a revised framework agreement extending the partnership to 2040. The Hygiene Austria LP GmbH joint venture (with Palmers Textil AG) settlement with the Austrian Cartel Court resulted in a fine of EUR 75,000 in 2024.
