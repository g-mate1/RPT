## Verbatim disclosure
[paraphrased] Related party transactions disclosed in the FY2024 consolidated financial statements include:

- **Majority shareholder relationship:** "The registered shares are held by SOF-11 Klimt CAI S.à r.l., Luxembourg, an entity managed by Starwood Capital Group, each granting the right to nominate one member of the Supervisory Board." Starwood (via SOF-11 Klimt CAI S.à r.l.) is the controlling shareholder; its stake stood at approximately 62.5% as of December 2024 (up from 57.9% in December 2021). [paraphrased from CA Immo Corporate Governance Report 2024 / Annual Report 2024]

- **Supervisory Board members representing Starwood:** Barry Sternlicht (Chairman, CEO of Starwood Capital), Jeffrey G. Dishner, Nicholas Chadwick (appointed effective 1 April 2025 via registered share) — all perform management functions within Starwood Capital Group. [paraphrased]

- **Supervisory Board remuneration:** Total remuneration of EUR 75K paid out in 2024 including attendance fees of EUR 15K (verbatim figure ranges quoted in snippets relate to remuneration paid in business year 2025 for service year 2024). Additional expenditure of EUR 81K in 2024 in connection with Supervisory Board (travel approx. EUR 25K, training/license costs EUR 39K, legal/consultancy approx. EUR 12-17K). No other fees (in particular for consultancy or brokerage activities) and no loans or advances were paid to Supervisory Board members. [paraphrased]

- **Management Board (KMP) compensation (FY2024):** Total salary payments to Management Board members in office EUR 3,242K (2023 comparative also stated). Fixed components EUR 1,156K consisting of: base salary EUR 1,035K, other benefits (cars, expense allowances, travel, holiday entitlements) EUR 42K, and pension fund contributions EUR 80K. Short-Term Incentive (STI) provisions of EUR 1,379K for FY2024. Long-Term Incentive (LTI) program provisions also disclosed. [paraphrased]

- **Business transactions with Supervisory Board members:** "Business transactions conducted between the company and members of the Supervisory Board who perform services outside of their Supervisory Board activities must conform to industry standards and be approved by the Supervisory Board." [paraphrased]

- **Dividend distributions to controlling shareholder:** A dividend of EUR 1.00 per share for FY2024 was proposed by the Management Board for the AGM on 5 May 2025. Prior special dividends initiated by the majority shareholder SOF-11 Klimt CAI: EUR 2.50/share (Dec 2021) + EUR 2.50/share (Mar 2022) totalling ~EUR 5/share (≈ EUR 490m gross), and EUR 2.56/share (≈ EUR 250m) special dividend resolved at the EGM on 10 Nov 2023 and paid in late 2023/early 2024. [paraphrased]

- **Subsidiaries / joint ventures / associates:** The consolidated financial statements include transactions with subsidiaries (eliminated on consolidation), and intra-group / joint-venture / associate transactions are disclosed as related party transactions in line with IAS 24. [paraphrased – exact amounts not retrievable]
