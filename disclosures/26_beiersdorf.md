## Verbatim disclosure
[paraphrased — verbatim retrieval blocked by 403 on issuer's site; the following reflects the content of the Related Party Disclosures note as reported via search-engine extracts from reports.beiersdorf.com]

Beiersdorf Aktiengesellschaft is controlled by maxingvest GmbH & Co. KGaA as the ultimate controlling party, and is included in that company's consolidated financial statements. Transactions and balances between Beiersdorf Aktiengesellschaft and those of its subsidiaries that qualify as related parties were largely eliminated in the course of consolidation and are not presented here.

Transactions with maxingvest GmbH & Co. KGaA and its affiliates (in particular the Tchibo Group):
- Purchase of goods and services from companies of the Tchibo Group.
- Rental of an office in Hong Kong from a company of the Tchibo Group.
- Expenses for settling invoices of a Tchibo Group company in Hong Kong, which were reimbursed.
- Beiersdorf Aktiengesellschaft and its associates and maxingvest GmbH & Co. KGaA and its associates pooled purchase volumes to achieve cost benefits, especially in the area of media procurement (print, TV, and digital advertising).
- Beiersdorf Shared Services GmbH provided IT services for TCHIBO GmbH in return for remuneration of approx. €3.3 million (previous year: approx. €2.9 million), and for Tchibo Coffee International Ltd. in return for remuneration of approx. €0.4 million.
- As at the reporting date, there were receivables from TCHIBO GmbH amounting to approx. €3.4 million (previous year: approx. €2.9 million) and from Tchibo Coffee International Ltd. amounting to approx. €0.9 million.

Transactions with associates:
- Various companies in the tesa Business Segment acquired goods from the associate Alkynes Co. Ltd., South Korea, for a total amount of approx. €13.4 million (previous year: approx. €11.7 million). Liabilities towards Alkynes Co. Ltd. amounted to approx. €1.7 million (previous year: approx. €1.7 million).

Transactions with non-consolidated subsidiaries:
- Beiersdorf Shared Services GmbH provided accounting and IT services to some of the non-consolidated subsidiaries.
- Beiersdorf Hautpflege GmbH operates two NIVEA Haus flagship locations; remuneration from Beiersdorf Aktiengesellschaft to Beiersdorf Hautpflege GmbH amounted to approx. €3.3 million (previous year: approx. €3.2 million).

Key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board) compensation:
- For 2024, total remuneration recognized as expenses pursuant to IFRS for all serving members of the Executive Board amounted to €23,609 thousand, of which fixed remuneration €5,400 thousand, ancillary benefits and other remuneration €3,336 thousand, the short-term Variable Bonus €2,379 thousand, and changes in provisions for the Long-Term Plan (LTP) €12,494 thousand.
- For the remuneration of the Executive Board, provisions of €2,708 thousand (previous year: €3,968 thousand) for the short-term variable remuneration and €39,727 thousand (previous year: €27,234 thousand) for the long-term variable remuneration were recognized at the reporting date.
- A total of €50,781 thousand (previous year: €50,845 thousand) has been accrued for pension obligations to former members of the Executive Board and their surviving dependents.
- The members of the Supervisory Board received remuneration totaling €1,714 thousand in fiscal year 2024.
