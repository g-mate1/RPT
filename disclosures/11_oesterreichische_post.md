Österreichische Post AG identifies the following categories of related parties under IAS 24:

(a) Shareholder with significant influence — Österreichische Beteiligungs AG ("ÖBAG"), which held 52.85% of the share capital as at 31 December 2024 and is wholly owned by the Republic of Austria. Through ÖBAG, the Republic of Austria is the ultimate controlling party.

(b) Other entities controlled, jointly controlled or significantly influenced by the Republic of Austria / ÖBAG — including Telekom Austria AG / A1 Group, Österreichische Bundesbahnen (ÖBB) Group, VERBUND AG, OMV AG, BIG (Bundesimmobiliengesellschaft), Casinos Austria, Post Bus / ÖBB Postbus, and other state-controlled entities. Austrian Post applies the IAS 24.25–27 exemption for transactions with government-related entities (Republic of Austria and its other controlled entities), and as such only individually significant transactions are disclosed; in the aggregate these transactions are of an ordinary, recurring commercial nature (electricity, telecommunications, rail/logistics services, real-estate leases).

(c) Subsidiaries — fully consolidated entities (eliminated on consolidation); the most material is bank99 AG (Austrian Post holds 100% following the buy-out of the former minority partners; the FY2024 figures reflect bank99 as a fully consolidated subsidiary providing retail banking via the post office network).

(d) Joint ventures and associates — including minority interests in logistics and parcel joint ventures (Aras Kargo associate in Turkey, AEP-Holding, and other joint operations).

(e) Key management personnel (KMP) — members of the Management Board (Vorstand) and the Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) of Österreichische Post AG. During FY2024 the Management Board comprised Georg Pölzl (Chairman/CEO until 30 September 2024), Walter Oblin (CFO; CEO from 1 October 2024) and Peter Umundum (Parcel & Logistics).

Transactions with these related parties are conducted at arm's length on customary market terms.

Specific items typically disclosed in the note include: services purchased from Telekom Austria/A1 (telecommunications), from ÖBB Group (rail freight, postal coach/Postbus services), and from VERBUND/energy-related state companies; receivables and payables in the ordinary course of business with the above; key management compensation broken down into short-term, post-employment, share-based and other long-term benefits; and remuneration of the Supervisory Board.

- Key management personnel (Management Board, Supervisory Board)
- Post-employment benefits / pension obligations to former Management Board members
