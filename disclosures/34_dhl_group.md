"In accordance with IAS 24, transactions between the Group and related parties must be reported. Related parties are defined, among others, as the Board of Management, the Supervisory Board and the members of their families. There were no reportable transactions or legal transactions involving these natural persons in the 2024 fiscal year."

"In addition to the consolidated subsidiaries, the Group has direct and indirect relationships with unconsolidated subsidiaries, investments accounted for using the equity method (associates and joint ventures), joint operations and other related entities in the course of its ordinary business activities. Transactions with these parties are conducted at arm's length and on the basis of standard market conditions."

"KfW (Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau), a public-law institution wholly owned by the Federal Republic of Germany, is the largest shareholder of Deutsche Post AG. On 6 February 2024 KfW sold 50 million shares of Deutsche Post AG; following the sale KfW held 16.45 %. After the capital reduction in May 2024 (cancellation of treasury shares), KfW's interest in the share capital of Deutsche Post AG increased again to 16.99 % as of 31 December 2024 (previous year: 20.49 %). The KfW holds these shares in trust to support the privatisation policy of the Federal Republic of Germany. The Group treats KfW (and other entities ultimately controlled by, or under significant influence of, the Federal Republic of Germany) as related parties under IAS 24 and applies the exemption available for government-related entities (IAS 24.25–27) for individually insignificant transactions conducted on standard market terms."

"The remuneration of key management personnel of the Group requiring disclosure under IAS 24 comprises the remuneration of the active members of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board. Detailed information by member is provided in the Remuneration Report, which is part of the combined management report."
