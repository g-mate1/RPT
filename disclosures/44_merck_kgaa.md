## Verbatim disclosure
[Source: Notes to the Consolidated Financial Statements — "Related party disclosures", Annual Report 2024 of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. Quotations are verbatim where shown in quotation marks; intervening or summarising text is marked [paraphrased].]

"Related parties in respect of the Group are E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, E. Merck Beteiligungen KG, Darmstadt, Germany, a related party of E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, and Emanuel-Merck-Vermögens-KG, Darmstadt, Germany, a related party of E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany. Furthermore, direct or indirect subsidiaries of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, associates of the Group, joint ventures of the Group, as well as pension funds that are classified as defined benefit plans [in accordance with IAS 19] are also related parties [within the meaning of IAS 24]."

"Members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, the Executive Board and the Board of Partners of E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, as well as close members of their families are also related parties, as are companies controlled or jointly controlled by this group of persons."

"The liabilities of Group companies in respect of E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, primarily resulted from mutual profit transfers between Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, and E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, as well as the profit transfer by Merck & Cie KmG, Altdorf, Switzerland, a subsidiary of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, to E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany. They also included financial liabilities of € 435.3 million (December 31, 2023: € 94.7 million) that were subject to standard market interest rates."

"The financial liabilities in respect of E. Merck Beteiligungen KG, Darmstadt, Germany, a related party of E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, in the amount of € 990.0 million (December 31, 2023: € 1,100.0 million) were also subject to standard market interest rates."

"No material activities between companies of the Group and members of the Executive Board or the Supervisory Board of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, the Executive Board or the Board of Partners of E. Merck KG, Darmstadt, Germany, or members of their immediate families took place in fiscal 2024 [or the previous year]."

[paraphrased] Information on Executive Board and Supervisory Board compensation is cross-referenced to Note (46) "Executive Board and Supervisory Board compensation". Per the Compensation Report, payments to former members of the Executive Board and their surviving dependents amounted to EUR 18.3 million in fiscal 2024 (prior year: EUR 14.4 million). Contractually agreed compensation of serving Executive Board members remained unchanged in fiscal 2024.

## Categories disclosed
- Identification of related parties: E. Merck KG (general partner / controlling shareholder ~70%), E. Merck Beteiligungen KG, Emanuel-Merck-Vermögens-KG, subsidiaries, associates, joint ventures, defined-benefit pension funds (IAS 19)
- Key management personnel: Executive Board and Supervisory Board of Merck KGaA; Executive Board and Board of Partners of E. Merck KG; close family members; entities controlled by them
- Mutual profit transfers between Merck KGaA and E. Merck KG (KGaA structure)
- Profit transfer by Merck & Cie KmG (Swiss subsidiary) to E. Merck KG
- Interest-bearing financial liabilities to E. Merck KG (EUR 435.3 m) and to E. Merck Beteiligungen KG (EUR 990.0 m) at standard market interest rates
- Statement that no material transactions occurred with KMP or their families in FY2024
- KMP compensation cross-reference to Note (46) and Compensation Report

## Quantitative highlights (EUR m)
- Sales to RP: not separately disclosed (no material trading activity beyond profit-transfer mechanism reported)
- Purchases from RP: not separately disclosed
- Receivables from RP: not separately disclosed in the note (immaterial / not quantified)
- Payables to RP (financial liabilities): EUR 435.3 m to E. Merck KG (2023: 94.7) + EUR 990.0 m to E. Merck Beteiligungen KG (2023: 1,100.0) = ~EUR 1,425.3 m at year-end 2024
- Loans from RP: included in the EUR 435.3 m and EUR 990.0 m figures above (interest-bearing at standard market terms)
- Profit transfer (mutual) Merck KGaA <-> E. Merck KG: occurred (amount not separately quantified in this note; reflected in the financial liabilities figure above and in the appropriation of profit)
- KMP compensation (Executive Board + Supervisory Board): see Note (46); payments to former Executive Board members and surviving dependents in FY2024 = EUR 18.3 m (2023: 14.4 m)
- Other transactions with members of the Executive/Supervisory Boards or Board of Partners or their families: none material in FY2024
