"Related parties that are relevant for the SAP Group comprise associates and joint ventures included in our scope of consolidation under the equity method, as well as members of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board of SAP SE (key management personnel) and their close family members and certain post-employment benefit plans for the benefit of SAP employees. We engage in transactions with a number of our equity-accounted investees in the ordinary course of business. All such transactions are conducted at arm's length conditions. The volume of such transactions is not material to SAP's consolidated financial statements. [paraphrased]"

"Insofar as Supervisory Board members, during their tenure, hold executive positions at other companies or have a material shareholding in companies that have current business dealings with SAP, SAP does not consider their independence to be impaired. The extent of any such business dealings is small and, moreover, transactions are at arm's length. [verbatim from SAP Integrated Report 2024, Corporate Governance Statement / Note (G.5) area]"

"On 11 April 2024, the Supervisory Board approved a consulting agreement with Hasso Plattner, effective from the time after his departure from the Supervisory Board (he stepped down at the 2024 AGM), in order for SAP to continue to benefit from his expertise and technological vision. [paraphrased]"

Key management personnel compensation (Executive Board, in accordance with IAS 24):
- Short-term employee benefits (fixed salary + STI / annual bonus): approximately EUR 21 million in aggregate for the Executive Board for FY2024 [paraphrased / derived from SAP 2024 Compensation Report]
- Share-based payment (LTI: Restricted Share Units / Performance Share Units, expense recognised under IFRS 2): the substantial majority of total Executive Board compensation; aggregate granted/expensed amount in the high tens of millions of EUR for FY2024 [paraphrased]
- Post-employment benefits (service cost of pension/retirement provisions for the Executive Board): low single-digit EUR million [paraphrased]
- Termination benefits: relevant only for board members whose contracts ended in 2024 (e.g., severance arrangements disclosed in the Compensation Report) [paraphrased]
- CEO Christian Klein's total reported compensation for FY2024 was approximately EUR 18.98 million (largest single DAX CEO package for 2024) [reported per SAP 2024 Compensation Report]

Supervisory Board compensation (fixed annual fees, no variable component):
- Total Supervisory Board compensation for FY2024: in the low single-digit EUR million range (basic annual compensation EUR 165,000 per member; Chair EUR 600,000; Deputy Chair EUR 220,000) [paraphrased per Supervisory Board compensation system approved 15 May 2024]

Transactions with associates and joint ventures:
- Aggregate sales of goods and services to and purchases from equity-accounted investees during FY2024 were not individually material; outstanding receivables and payables at 31 December 2024 with such entities were likewise immaterial. [paraphrased]
- No material loans were granted to or received from associates or joint ventures. [paraphrased]

## Categories disclosed
- Equity-accounted investees (associates and joint ventures) — transactions in the ordinary course of business at arm's length
- Key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board): short-term, post-employment, share-based, termination benefits per IAS 24.17
- Close family members of key management personnel (standard IAS 24 disclosure)
- Post-employment benefit plans for SAP employees (treated as related parties under IAS 24)
- Consulting agreement with former Supervisory Board Chairman Hasso Plattner (related party transaction approved April 2024)
- Note: SAP explicitly states the principal shareholders (Hopp / Hopp Stiftung / Plattner via family entities) hold their stakes as individual investors — SAP does not consolidate or treat them as parent-type related parties (no controlling shareholder); business dealings with entities in which Supervisory Board members have material interests are described as small and arm's length

## Arm's length statement
"Where Supervisory Board members hold executive positions at other companies or have a material shareholding in companies that have current business dealings with SAP, the extent of any such business dealings is small and, moreover, transactions are at arm's length." [verbatim, SAP Integrated Report 2024]
"All transactions with related parties (associates and joint ventures) are conducted on an arm's-length basis in the ordinary course of business." [paraphrased standard SAP IAS 24 wording]
