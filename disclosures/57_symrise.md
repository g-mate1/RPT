Symrise AG is a widely-held listed company (DAX); no single shareholder exercises control. In addition, as in the previous year, two joint ventures and eight associates were recognized at equity in the consolidated financial statements of Symrise AG for FY2024. Related parties of the Symrise Group within the meaning of IAS 24 therefore comprise (i) the members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of Symrise AG (key management personnel, KMP), their close family members; and (ii) joint ventures and associated companies accounted for using the equity method, together with non-consolidated subsidiaries.

[paraphrased] Transactions with associated companies and joint ventures (including the Vizag Care Ingredients Private Limited JV with the Virchow Group in India and other equity-accounted investees) are conducted in the ordinary course of business on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions.

[paraphrased] Compensation of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board (key management personnel) for FY2024 is disclosed in detail in the separate Remuneration Report of Symrise AG. The CEO transition occurred during the year: Dr. Heinz-Jürgen Bertram left the Executive Board on March 31, 2024 and entered retirement (fixed monthly salary continued under contract until October 31, 2025; fixed pension payments in FY2024 amounted to EUR 122,459.67 gross from April 1, 2024). Dr. Jean-Yves Parisot succeeded him as CEO on April 1, 2024. Short-term Incentive (STI), Long-Term Incentive Plan (LTIP 2024–2027) and pension obligations to former Executive Board members are recognized in accordance with IAS 19 / IAS 24.

[paraphrased] Receivables from and liabilities to related parties (associates and joint ventures) at the reporting date are disclosed in the related-party note; no material loans, guarantees or off-balance-sheet commitments to related parties were reported.
