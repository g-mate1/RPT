[paraphrased] "Related parties of the Sartorius Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise (i) the members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of Sartorius AG and their close family members (key management personnel, "KMP"), (ii) the shareholders that exercise joint control or significant influence over Sartorius AG — namely the members of the Sartorius family pool of shareholders (acting through Sartorius-Herbst Beteiligungen I GmbH and Sartorius-Herbst Beteiligungen II GmbH and related private holding vehicles) and Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc. — and (iii) non-consolidated subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures of the Sartorius Group.

Sartorius Stedim Biotech S.A. (SSB), Aubagne (France), is fully consolidated within the Sartorius Group and is therefore not a related party of the Group at the consolidated level; intra-group transactions with SSB and its subsidiaries are eliminated on consolidation. SSB is, however, a related party in the separate financial statements of Sartorius AG.

In the ordinary course of business, Group companies sold goods and services to, and purchased goods and services from, Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc. and its subsidiaries. Such transactions were carried out on an arm's-length basis at prices and conditions equivalent to those agreed with unrelated third parties and were of minor significance to the Group. They are typically handled on a purchase-order basis.

Transactions with the Sartorius family pool of shareholders and entities controlled by them, as well as with non-consolidated subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures, were likewise of minor significance and were conducted on an arm's-length basis.

The remuneration of the members of the Executive Board and of the Supervisory Board of Sartorius AG (key management personnel) is disclosed in the Remuneration Report, which forms part of the combined management report. KMP compensation consists of short-term employee benefits (fixed remuneration, short-term variable remuneration, fringe benefits), post-employment benefits (additions to pension provisions / service cost), share-based / long-term variable remuneration (multi-year performance share plan tranches), and, where applicable, termination benefits. No loans or guarantees were granted to members of the Executive Board or the Supervisory Board in the financial year."
