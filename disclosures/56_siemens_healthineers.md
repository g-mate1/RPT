**[paraphrased]** Siemens AG, Munich, Germany, was the ultimate controlling shareholder of Siemens Healthineers AG throughout fiscal year 2024, holding approximately 75% of the outstanding ordinary shares. Accordingly, Siemens AG and all of its subsidiaries, joint ventures, and associated companies outside the Siemens Healthineers consolidation perimeter, as well as the Siemens Pension-Trust e.V. and equivalent foreign pension trustees, qualify as related parties under IAS 24. In addition, the members of the Managing Board and Supervisory Board of Siemens Healthineers AG, together with their close family members, qualify as key management personnel.

**[paraphrased]** Transactions with the Siemens Group cover a broad range of recurring goods and services. These include: (i) sales of medical-technology products and services to Siemens AG and its subsidiaries; (ii) purchases of IT/digital services, shared services and other corporate functions (including treasury support, real-estate services, tax/legal services and Siemens-branded software platforms) provided through Siemens Global Business Services and other Siemens entities; (iii) license fees paid for the use of the "Siemens" trademark under a long-term trademark license agreement with Siemens Trademark GmbH & Co. KG; (iv) participation in the Siemens cash-pooling, intercompany loan and hedging arrangements operated through Siemens Treasury; (v) leases of real estate from Siemens Real Estate; and (vi) services connected with the Siemens pension trust structures (Siemens Pension-Trust e.V. and equivalents).

**[paraphrased]** All transactions with related parties are stated to be conducted on an arm's-length basis (at conditions equivalent to those that would prevail in transactions with unrelated third parties).

**[paraphrased]** Key management personnel compensation comprises short-term benefits (fixed and variable cash), share-based payments, post-employment benefits and other long-term benefits, and is disclosed in aggregate together with individualized disclosure in the separate Compensation Report 2024.
