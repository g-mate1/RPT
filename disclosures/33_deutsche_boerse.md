## Verbatim disclosure
[paraphrased — verbatim not retrievable; structure consistent with prior years' Deutsche Börse Group IAS 24 note]

Related parties of Deutsche Börse Group within the meaning of IAS 24 are (a) fully consolidated subsidiaries (transactions and balances eliminated on consolidation and not separately disclosed), (b) associates and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method, (c) other investees that are not consolidated, (d) key management personnel (KMP) — namely the members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of Deutsche Börse AG and close members of their families, and (e) post-employment benefit plans for the benefit of employees of Group companies (Deutsche Börse pension trust e.V. / contractual trust arrangements).

Transactions with associates and joint ventures relate primarily to the provision of trading, clearing, settlement, IT and other infrastructure services by Deutsche Börse Group entities, and to the receipt of services from these equity-accounted investees. The Group's principal associates and joint ventures include — among others — Tradegate Exchange GmbH (associate, ~43% stake), European Energy Exchange-related minorities/joint operations not yet fully consolidated, Digital Vault Services / regulatory reporting joint ventures and EuroCTP (joint venture formed in 2023 with other European bourses). [paraphrased]

Transactions and balances with related parties are conducted on an arm's length basis at conditions that would also apply to transactions with third parties. [paraphrased — Deutsche Börse has included this standard arm's length statement in prior years' related-party notes and the disclosure is reaffirmed by the corporate governance statement's reference to the section 111a (2) sentence 2 AktG controls on related-party transactions.]

Compensation of key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board) is disclosed in aggregate by component (short-term benefits, post-employment benefits, share-based payments and termination benefits); the detailed individualised remuneration is set out in the separate Remuneration Report which forms part of the FY2024 Annual Report and is submitted for approval at the 2025 AGM.
