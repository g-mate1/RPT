Paraphrased substance of the FY2024 Related Party note:

"Related parties of the BMW Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise non-consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates of the BMW Group, the members of the Board of Management and of the Supervisory Board of BMW AG, the close family members of those persons, and entities controlled or significantly influenced by such persons. In particular, Stefan Quandt and Susanne Klatten (Dr h.c.) — both members of the Supervisory Board of BMW AG and, taken together, holders of the majority of voting rights in BMW AG — and the entities controlled by them (including AQTON SE, SOLIDAS Vermögensverwaltungs GmbH / Solid Capital, SKion GmbH and Altana AG and their subsidiaries) are classified as related parties.

Following the acquisition of an additional 25% interest with effect from February 2022, BMW Brilliance Automotive Ltd. (BBA, Shenyang) is a fully consolidated subsidiary of the BMW Group and is therefore no longer a related party of the Group; transactions with BBA are eliminated on consolidation.

Significant joint ventures and associates accounted for using the equity method include, among others, Spotlight Automotive Ltd. (50/50 joint operation with Great Wall Motor for production of all-electric MINI vehicles in Zhangjiagang, China), Beijing Ionchi New Energy Technology Ltd. (50/50 joint venture with Mercedes-Benz for a public fast-charging network in China, completed 4 March 2024) and BMW TechWorks India Pvt. Ltd. (50/50 joint venture with Tata Technologies for automotive software and business IT, completed 8 October 2024), together with the Group's other joint ventures and associates listed in the schedule of shareholdings (e.g. DriveNow / SIXT, Critical Raw Materials, IONITY GmbH, There Holding B.V., YourNow, ChargeNow joint operations etc.).

Transactions between BMW AG / its consolidated subsidiaries and these joint ventures, associates and non-consolidated subsidiaries comprise the supply of vehicles, parts and components, the provision of engineering and other services, the granting and receipt of loans within the Group financing pool, and the receipt of dividends.

Transactions with members of the Board of Management and of the Supervisory Board and their close family members, and with entities controlled or significantly influenced by them, were not material in the financial year 2024 and the previous financial year. No loans or advances were granted to members of the Board of Management or the Supervisory Board in the financial year 2024. Members of the Board of Management or the Supervisory Board, or related entities, did not render services to the BMW Group outside their Board mandates beyond an immaterial extent.

Key management personnel of the BMW Group are the members of the Board of Management and of the Supervisory Board of BMW AG. Their total remuneration in the financial year 2024 is presented in detail in the Remuneration Report 2024 (BMW Group Report 2024, Section 05), which forms part of the BMW Group Report. The total expense recognised in respect of key management personnel covers short-term benefits (fixed salary, fringe benefits, annual bonus / STI), post-employment benefits (service cost for pension entitlements), other long-term benefits and share-based payments (long-term incentive — RSU programme).

All transactions with related parties were conducted on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's length transactions."
