The following summary is based on Commerzbank's recurring IAS 24 disclosure structure as applied in its consolidated annual reports, together with publicly confirmed FY2024 facts (ownership: Federal Republic of Germany ~12% via the Finanzagentur/FMS; UniCredit S.p.A. built a significant stake during H2 2024; KPMG as group auditor for FY2024).]

Pursuant to IAS 24, persons and companies controlled or significantly influenced by Commerzbank Aktiengesellschaft, as well as persons and companies that exercise control or significant influence over Commerzbank Aktiengesellschaft, are deemed to be related parties. Related parties of the Commerzbank Group therefore comprise:

- The Federal Republic of Germany, represented by the Bundesrepublik Deutschland — Finanzagentur GmbH (which holds the residual Financial Market Stabilisation Fund / FMS shareholding of approximately 12%), and companies controlled by or under significant influence of the Federal Republic of Germany (government-related entities under IAS 24.25);
- Subsidiaries not consolidated due to immateriality;
- Joint ventures and associated companies (in particular those accounted for using the equity method);
- Members of the Board of Managing Directors and the Supervisory Board of Commerzbank AG ("key management personnel") and their close family members, plus companies controlled or jointly controlled by these persons;
- Post-employment benefit plans for the benefit of Commerzbank Group employees (in particular pension trusts / CTAs).

In the course of ordinary banking business, transactions are entered into with related parties. These include in particular loans, deposits, derivatives, guarantees and ordinary banking services (payments, securities settlement, custody, asset management). All such transactions are conducted on an arm's length basis (at market conditions and standard terms).

Transactions with the Federal Republic of Germany and German federal-government-controlled entities (e.g. KfW Bankengruppe, Deutsche Bahn AG, Deutsche Telekom AG and other government-related entities) are disclosed in accordance with the partial exemption for government-related entities under IAS 24.25-27: Commerzbank avails itself of the reduced disclosure requirements and discloses the nature and approximate amount of any individually significant transactions, plus a qualitative indication of the extent of collectively significant transactions. In the ordinary course of business Commerzbank holds debt securities issued by the Federal Republic of Germany and by other German public-sector issuers, conducts repo / reverse-repo transactions, money-market and derivative transactions with KfW and other public-sector counterparties, and provides standard banking services.

UniCredit S.p.A. — which announced and built a significant equity stake in Commerzbank during September 2024 — is, depending on the cumulative voting rights actually held at the reporting date and the absence of board representation, generally NOT classified by Commerzbank as exercising significant influence under IAS 28 / IAS 24; accordingly, in the FY2024 consolidated financial statements UniCredit is treated as a substantial shareholder rather than as an IAS 24 related party. Key management personnel compensation is disclosed in aggregate by component (short-term employee benefits, post-employment benefits, other long-term benefits, termination benefits, share-based payment). Detailed individual figures for members of the Board of Managing Directors and the Supervisory Board are presented in the Remuneration Report, which is audited separately by KPMG pursuant to §162 AktG.

Loans and advances to members of the Board of Managing Directors and the Supervisory Board, as well as related contingent liabilities (guarantees), are disclosed separately; balances are immaterial relative to Group total assets. No write-downs were recognised on receivables from related parties. [paraphrased]

- Receivables / loans to related parties (non-consolidated subs, JVs, associates, KMP combined): present but individually and collectively immaterial relative to Group total assets (~EUR 555 bn).
- Payables / deposits from related parties: immaterial relative to Group customer deposits.
- Transactions with the Federal Republic of Germany and government-related entities: disclosed under the IAS 24.25 partial exemption — primarily holdings of German government and government-agency debt securities and money-market / derivative transactions with KfW and other federal entities, conducted at arm's length.

- Loans/guarantees to Board members and Supervisory Board members: immaterial.
- No bad-debt expense or impairment loss recognised on related-party receivables.
