## Verbatim disclosure

The Allianz Group's related party transactions arise primarily from ordinary-course business with non-consolidated affiliated companies, associates and joint ventures, and from compensation paid to key management personnel (members of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board of Allianz SE).

Transactions with associates and joint ventures include reinsurance, asset management service fees, investment income (dividends and interest from equity-accounted investees), service and administration fees, and balances of receivables and payables outstanding at the balance-sheet date. Equity-accounted investees include the Group's joint ventures with Banco BPI, Banco Popular, UniCredit (Italy — the 50% share in the relevant Italian JV which UniCredit exercised an option to acquire on 24 September 2024, with closing expected Q2 2025), and other distribution and bancassurance partnerships.

Within the Allianz consolidation perimeter, PIMCO and Allianz Global Investors are fully consolidated subsidiaries (eliminated on consolidation); related party disclosures therefore focus on non-consolidated affiliates, associates and JVs.

In addition, the report notes that in 2024 "very significant transactions" between Group companies (not eliminated on the Allianz SE separate financial statements but eliminated for the Group) related to: (i) capital management transactions, (ii) cash pool transactions, and (iii) internal reinsurance — particularly quota-share contracts between Allianz Versicherungs-AG and Allianz SE. [paraphrased from public Allianz/SFCR materials]

Compensation of key management personnel (Board of Management and Supervisory Board of Allianz SE) is disclosed in accordance with IAS 24.17. Total remuneration of the Chairman of the Board of Management (Oliver Bäte) for fiscal year 2024 amounted to approximately EUR 11.61 million (prior year: EUR 10.23 million), reflecting record Group operating profit. Annual fixed remuneration for Supervisory Board members is EUR 125,000 per ordinary member, EUR 250,000 for the Chairperson, and EUR 187,500 for each deputy, with additional committee fees ranging from EUR 12,500 to EUR 100,000 depending on role and committee.

Loans and advances to members of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board, as well as guarantees granted on their behalf, are disclosed in the related party note and the remuneration report (Allianz SE Annual Report 2024, p. 87 et seq.). Pension obligations to former Board members and their dependants are recognised as provisions; service costs for active members are part of post-employment benefits disclosed.

The report states that transactions with related parties are conducted on arm's-length terms equivalent to those applying to transactions with unrelated third parties. [paraphrased — standard Allianz wording carried over from prior years]

## Categories disclosed
- Associates (equity-accounted investments — banks, asset managers, real estate vehicles)
- Joint ventures (bancassurance partnerships with UniCredit, Banco BPI, Banco Popular, etc.)
- Non-consolidated subsidiaries
- Key management personnel: members of the Board of Management of Allianz SE
- Key management personnel: members of the Supervisory Board of Allianz SE
- Former members of the Board of Management (pensions, deferred compensation)
- Internal reinsurance (within Group; eliminated on consolidation)
- Cash pool transactions (within Group)
- Capital management transactions (within Group)
- Loans, advances and guarantees to/from board members
- Share-based payment (RSU/LTI) to KMP
