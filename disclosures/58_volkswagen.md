> "Porsche Automobil Holding SE, Stuttgart, held 53.3% of the voting rights of Volkswagen AG as of the reporting date. Porsche SE has the power to participate in the operating policy decisions of the Volkswagen Group and is therefore classified as a related party as defined by IAS 24."
> "As part of the IPO of Porsche AG and the sale of ordinary shares to Porsche SE in fiscal year 2022, Porsche SE and Volkswagen AG entered into [...] a 'procedural and amendment agreement and agreement to amend the Comprehensive Agreement'."
