"Note 24 — Related Party Transactions. We have ongoing commercial relationships with companies in which we hold a non-controlling equity interest accounted for under the equity method, principally our 50%-owned joint venture PreAnalytiX GmbH (a joint venture with Becton, Dickinson and Company), and with certain other equity-method investees. Transactions with these related parties are conducted in the ordinary course of business on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions.

Net sales to related parties for the years ended December 31, 2024, 2023 and 2022 were $3,073 thousand, $9,039 thousand and $8,474 thousand, respectively. As of December 31, 2024, the Company's investment in PreAnalytiX GmbH (a 50% joint venture with BD) was $3,965 thousand (2023: comparable balance reported). Transactions with PreAnalytiX during 2024 amounted to $4,344 thousand (2023: $4,977 thousand).

In 2022, we fully reserved a $10.6 million loan receivable from a related party (a non-consolidated investee), and in 2023 the defaulted loan was written off against the reserve. No new loans to related parties were issued in 2024 and no additional impairments were recognized.

Compensation of key management personnel (members of the Managing Board and the Supervisory Board) is disclosed in the Compensation section (Item 6.B of the Form 20-F) and in the 2024 Remuneration Report; it comprises base salary, short-term incentive (STI) cash bonus, long-term equity-based incentive (LTI/RSU/PSU) awards, pension and other benefits. For 2024, total at-target compensation for the CEO and CFO remained unchanged versus 2023, with no change to base salary, target STI bonus percentage or LTI target. [...]"
