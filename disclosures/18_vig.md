VIG's controlling shareholder is **Wiener Städtische Wechselseitiger Versicherungsverein – Vermögensverwaltung – Vienna Insurance Group ("WSTW", the mutual association)**, which holds approximately 72% of VIG's share capital and is identified as the ultimate parent / main shareholder. WSTW provides D&O and casualty insurance covering the top management level of the VIG Group. [paraphrased]

Related parties of VIG AG Wiener Versicherung Gruppe comprise: (i) the parent mutual WSTW and its subsidiaries / sister entities outside the consolidation scope; (ii) unconsolidated subsidiaries of VIG; (iii) associates and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method; (iv) key management personnel (Managing Board and Supervisory Board of VIG AG) and their close family members; and (v) post-employment benefit plans for the benefit of VIG employees. The strategic banc­assurance partner Erste Group Bank AG is not a related party of VIG under IAS 24 (no significant influence relationship), but is a significant counterparty: in 2024 premiums generated via Erste Group banks amounted to ~EUR 1.43 billion (+6% YoY). [paraphrased]
