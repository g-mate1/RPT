Ownership / control structure. PIERER Mobility AG is a listed Austrian stock corporation (ATX prime; SIX Swiss Exchange; Vienna Stock Exchange). Its majority shareholder is Pierer Bajaj AG (PBAG), Wels, Austria, holding approximately 74.9% of the shares. PBAG itself is held 50.1% by Pierer Industrie AG, Wels (whose sole indirect shareholder is Stefan Pierer, also Executive Board member and former CEO of PIERER Mobility AG), and 49.9% by BAIH B.V. (Bajaj Auto International Holdings B.V.), a wholly owned subsidiary of Bajaj Auto Ltd., Pune, India. The ultimate parent of the group is Pierer Konzerngesellschaft mbH, Wels, Austria. The financial statements of PIERER Mobility AG are included in the consolidated financial statements of Pierer Konzerngesellschaft mbH, which prepares the largest set of consolidated financial statements in the group. [paraphrased]

Identified related parties of the PIERER Mobility Group in FY2024 included: (i) the ultimate parent Pierer Konzerngesellschaft mbH and other companies in the Pierer Konzerngesellschaft / Pierer Industrie sub-group (including Pierer Industrie AG and its subsidiaries such as PIERER Innovation GmbH, PIERER Immoreal GmbH, KTM Immobilien GmbH (prior to deconsolidation events), PIERER Green Energy GmbH and others); (ii) the principal shareholder Pierer Bajaj AG; (iii) Bajaj Auto Ltd., Pune, India and its affiliates (significant shareholder via BAIH B.V.); (iv) members of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board of PIERER Mobility AG and their close family members; (v) associates and joint ventures of the Group, including (until deconsolidation in late November 2024) KTM AG and its subsidiaries — which entered Austrian judicial restructuring proceedings with self-administration on 29 November 2024 and were subsequently deconsolidated from the PIERER Mobility consolidation scope in FY2024 — as well as MV Agusta Motor S.p.A. (Varese, Italy, 50.1% acquired in two stages, controlled by KTM AG / KTM Group prior to KTM deconsolidation). [paraphrased]

Restructuring and KTM deconsolidation. In November 2024, KTM AG, together with KTM Components GmbH and KTM Forschungs & Entwicklungs GmbH, filed for judicial restructuring proceedings with self-administration. As a consequence, KTM AG and its subsidiaries were deconsolidated in the FY2024 consolidated financial statements of PIERER Mobility AG. After the reporting date, in 2025, Bajaj provided funds to PIERER Mobility AG and to KTM AG/KTM Group entities to serve the quotas under the KTM restructuring proceedings; these transactions with Bajaj Auto Ltd. / BAIH B.V. (related party of PIERER Mobility AG via PBAG) are disclosed as related-party transactions and as subsequent events. [paraphrased]

Significant FY2024 related-party transactions specifically disclosed include:
- Acquisition of PB Invest GmbH by KTM Components GmbH from Pierer Industrie AG, Wels, by a legally effective contract dated 2 September 2024. PB Invest GmbH was a holding company whose only significant asset was a 100% interest in Vöcklabrucker Metallgießerei GmbH; PB Invest was merged with KTM Components GmbH on acquisition and Vöcklabrucker Metallgießerei GmbH became a fully consolidated subsidiary of the KTM Group. [paraphrased]
- Acquisition of PIERER Green Energy GmbH by PIERER Industrie AG, Wels with effect from 1 June 2024 (subsequently merged with KTM Immobilien GmbH). Purchase price approximately EUR 0.3 million. [paraphrased]
- Transfer of the new-mobility products business bundled in the PIERER New Mobility GmbH sub-group from the KTM Group to PIERER Mobility AG with effect from 1 June 2024. As at 31 December 2024, KTM AG had a financial receivable from PIERER New Mobility GmbH amounting to EUR 372.0 million. [paraphrased]
- Acquisition of the remaining shares in MV Agusta Motor S.p.A. (Varese, Italy) under an early-exercised call option agreement completed in March 2024, with an agreed purchase price of EUR 45 million (EUR 35 million in cash plus EUR 10 million in PIERER Mobility AG shares) plus a subsequent purchase price payment of at least EUR 5 million. [paraphrased]
- Goods and services exchanged in the ordinary course of business with companies of the Pierer Industrie / Pierer Konzerngesellschaft sub-group, including management services, IT services, real-estate rental (leases of administrative and production properties), insurance services and cost allocations. [paraphrased]
- Trading transactions and royalty / licence and supply arrangements with Bajaj Auto Ltd. (sale of components and finished products, royalty and licence flows in connection with the joint product development cooperation for small-displacement motorcycles). [paraphrased]

Key Management Personnel (KMP) compensation. Total remuneration paid to the Executive Board of PIERER Mobility AG for the FY2024 financial year amounted to EUR 2,607,281.17. As the criteria for variable (performance-based) remuneration were not met for FY2024, the total remuneration consists exclusively of fixed remuneration (no variable component was paid). The Executive Board as at 31 December 2024 comprised Stefan Pierer (CEO) and Gottfried Neumeister (Co-CEO, appointed 1 September 2024); the previous members Florian Kecht, Alex Pierer, Hubert Trunkenpolz and Rudolf Wiesbeck left the Executive Board on 31 October 2024. Supervisory Board remuneration is disclosed separately. [paraphrased]

Arm's length / pricing statement. All transactions with related parties were conducted on terms that correspond to those that would apply between independent third parties (at arm's length). [paraphrased]
