## 2. DE statutory threshold under § 111b (1) AktG
- **Threshold = 1.5% × Konzern-Bilanzsumme = 1.5% × EUR 51,567 m ≈ EUR 773.5 million**
- A single RPT (or aggregate within FY with one related party) must reach > EUR ~774 m to trigger Aufsichtsrat-Zustimmung and § 111c-Bekanntmachung.

## 4. Threshold breach? — **N (No, with caveat)**
- The EUR 990.0 m financial liability to E. Merck Beteiligungen KG exceeds 1.5% of Bilanzsumme **as a year-end stock figure**, but § 111b/111c AktG addresses **transactions (Geschäfte)**, not standing balances. The underlying loan was originated in prior years (carrying value 2023: EUR 1,100.0 m, i.e. partially repaid in 2024) and represents the **continuation** of existing inter-company financing at arm's-length interest rates — not a new "Geschäft" within FY2024 reaching the 1.5% threshold.
- Mutual profit transfers under the KGaA-Komplementär structure are part of the partnership-law profit appropriation mechanism and are typically treated as outside the scope of § 111a Abs. 2 AktG (transactions in the ordinary course of business and on customary market terms / inherent in the legal form).
- No discrete FY2024 RPT exceeding EUR 774 m has been identified that would have required § 111b approval / § 111c publication.
- **Net result: no § 111c-Bekanntmachung triggered for FY2024.**
