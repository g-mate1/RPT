## 2. German SRD II threshold (§ 111b AktG)

- **Statutory rule:** § 111b (1) AktG — Supervisory Board consent and § 111c AktG announcement required for any RPT (or aggregate of RPTs with the same related party within one financial year, § 111a (2) AktG) whose value exceeds **1.5 % of the sum of fixed and current assets** of the parent company under § 266 (2) A and B HGB on the IFRS consolidated balance sheet basis used here for proxy purposes.
- **Threshold computation:**

  | Calculation | Value |
  |---|---|
  | 1.5 % × EUR 12,497 m | **EUR 187.5 m** |

- Any individual RPT (or 12-month aggregate with the same related party) ≥ **EUR ~187 m** would trigger Supervisory Board approval and public announcement under § 111c AktG.

## 3. Largest disclosed RPT in FY2024 vs. threshold

MTU's IAS 24 note discloses ongoing trading with equity-accounted JVs:
- **MTU Maintenance Zhuhai Co., Ltd.** (China; 50/50 with China Southern Airlines) — largest by volume; spare parts/module sales and MRO purchases
- **EME Aero Sp. z o.o.** (Poland; 50/50 with Lufthansa Technik) — GTF MRO shop; second largest
- **EUMET S.A.S.** (50/50 with Safran Aircraft Engines; NGF/FCAS program)
- **EURA** (50/50 with Safran Helicopter Engines, newly formed June 2024)
- **Airfoil Services Sdn. Bhd.** (associate, Malaysia)

Magnitudes (per disclosure file, paraphrased FY2024): sales to JVs in the low triple-digit EUR m range, purchases similarly. The single largest counterparty (MTU Maintenance Zhuhai) likely sits in the high-double-digit / low-triple-digit EUR m range on a gross sales basis; on a net 12-month aggregate basis it remains well **below** the EUR 187.5 m threshold (MTU's published numbers in FY2023 disclosure pointed to JV sales of ca. EUR 100-150 m in aggregate, allocated across four JVs). No single related party transaction or 12-month aggregate disclosed in FY2024 reaches 1.5 % of consolidated total assets.
