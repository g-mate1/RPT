## 2. DE statutory threshold under § 111b (1) AktG
- **Threshold = 1.5% × Konzern-Bilanzsumme = 1.5% × EUR 232 bn ≈ EUR 3.48 bn**
- A single related-party transaction must reach > EUR ~3.5 bn (cumulative within FY) to trigger Aufsichtsrat approval and § 111c publication.

## 3. Largest disclosed RPT (FY2024)
- **None individually quantified in the IAS 24 note** at a level that approaches the § 111b threshold.
- Largest IAS 24 category disclosed: KMP compensation (Vorstand + Aufsichtsrat) — low double-digit EUR m (well below threshold).
- Transactions with associates/JVs (Tradegate Exchange, EuroCTP, Digital Vault Services etc.) are at arm's length and in the low single-digit EUR m range — orders of magnitude below EUR 3.48 bn.

## 4. Threshold breach? — **N (No)**
- No FY2024 RPT exceeds 1.5% of consolidated Bilanzsumme.
- Consequently no § 111b Aufsichtsrats-Zustimmung and no § 111c-Bekanntmachung is triggered.
