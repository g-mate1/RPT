# Porsche Automobil Holding SE — SRD II Schwellenwertanalyse FY2024

**Index:** DAX · **Country:** Germany · **Period:** FY2024
**Legal basis:** §§ 111a-111c AktG (German implementation of SRD II, Art. 9c)
**Threshold rule (Germany):** 1.5% of the sum of fixed and current assets per § 111b (1) AktG → 1.5% × Bilanzsumme (consolidated total assets, IFRS)

| Item | EUR million |

Source: Porsche SE Annual Report 2024 (consolidated statement of financial position), https://www.porsche-se.com/fileadmin/downloads/investorrelations/mandatorypublications/annualreport-24/PSE2024_Annual_Report_en.pdf

Note: The sharp YoY decrease reflects the non-cash impairment of the carrying amounts of the investments in Volkswagen AG and Porsche AG (ad-hoc release of 22 Jan 2025), which materially reduced the equity-accounted investment line and thereby Bilanzsumme. The DE Schwelle is computed off the post-impairment FY2024 closing balance.

## 2. DE-Schwelle (1.5%)

| | EUR million |
|---|---|
| 1.5% × 42,841 | **≈ 642.6** |

Any single RPT (or aggregate of RPTs with the same party within the trailing 12 months, § 111a (1) sentence 2 AktG) with a value at or above ~€643 m triggers the § 111b approval and § 111c publication duty.

Exemptions relevant here (§ 111a (2)-(3) AktG):
- (i) Transactions in the ordinary course of business and on arm's-length terms (Independent-Committee finding required) — covered by Porsche SE's standing IAS 24 arm's-length statement.
- (ii) Transactions with wholly-owned subsidiaries — n/a (operating subgroups VW/Porsche AG are not wholly owned).
- (iii) Transactions concerning remuneration / KMP compensation — exempt.
- (iv) Dividends distributed pro rata to all shareholders — **exempt**. This is decisive for Porsche SE because its largest cash flows from related parties are dividends.

**Largest single related-party cash flow:** dividend of ≈ €1,400 m from Volkswagen AG (>2x the 1.5% threshold in absolute terms), but **exempt from § 111c publication** because pro-rata dividends to all shareholders are carved out of the SRD II / AktG RPT regime.

**Largest non-exempt RPT:** none disclosed reaches the €643 m threshold. Porsche SE is a pure holding; operating receivables/payables sit inside the VW and Porsche AG sub-consolidations and are not Porsche-SE-level RPTs.
