## 2. SRD II / § 111b AktG materiality threshold (DE: 1.5%)

> **Schwelle = 1.5% × 111,361 EUR m = ~1,670 EUR m (~ EUR 1.67 bn)**

A single transaction (or a series of transactions with the same related party within a 12-month period, not previously approved/published) whose value reaches or exceeds this threshold triggers:
- mandatory Supervisory Board approval (§ 111b AktG), and
- public announcement on the company's website within the meaning of § 111c AktG (typically distributed Europe-wide via EQS Newswire).

## 3. Largest disclosed RPT in Note (33) — IAS 24 perspective

E.ON SE's Note (33) "Transactions with Related Parties" in the FY2024 consolidated financial statements does **not** present any single transaction at or above the EUR 1.67 bn § 111b AktG threshold on a stand-alone basis. The largest related-party exposure disclosed in the IAS 24 note is the **ongoing commercial relationship with RWE AG / RWE Group** (RWE holds ~15% of E.ON shares since the 2019/2020 asset swap), comprising electricity procurement/sales, network access and continuing settlement of swap-related arrangements. These flows are aggregated as recurring, arm's-length, ordinary-course transactions and are therefore exempt from § 111c publication under § 111a(2) AktG (ordinary-course-of-business / market-conditions exemption with Supervisory Board procedure).

Other related-party categories disclosed (associates/JVs, CTA pension vehicle, KMP compensation) are individually well below the threshold.

> **Largest disclosed RPT:** aggregate transactions with the **RWE Group** (significant shareholder, ~15%) — disclosed as recurring ordinary-course arm's-length flows; no single sub-transaction reaches the 1.5% threshold based on the publicly available note.
