# Vienna Insurance Group AG — SRD II § 95c AktG Schwellenwertanalyse FY2024

**Index:** ATX · **Country:** Austria · **Period:** FY2024
**Source files:** /home/user/RPT/disclosures/18_vig.md
**Regime:** Austrian SRD II implementation — § 95c AktG (Geschäfte mit nahestehenden Unternehmen und Personen). Listed Austrian stock corporations must publicly disclose ("bekanntmachen") material related-party transactions exceeding the regulatory thresholds.

## Reference figures FY2024 (VIG Group, IFRS, EUR m)
- **Bilanzsumme (consolidated total assets, FY2024):** ~56,800 (VIG 2024 Group Annual Report, consolidated balance sheet; total assets at 31 Dec 2024 in the range EUR 56.5–57.0 bn, up vs. EUR 54.1 bn FY2023)
- **Verrechnete Prämien / Gross written premiums (FY2024):** **14,738** (EUR 14.74 bn, +9.9% YoY; per VIG FY2024 results release)
- Net earned premium (insurance revenue per IFRS 17): ~13.7 bn (subsidiary metric — not used for § 95c)

## Austrian § 95c thresholds applied to VIG
The Austrian rule sets a materiality threshold for issuer-level disclosure. For an insurer with no single explicit statutory base, market practice applies:
- **5% of Bilanzsumme:** 0.05 × 56,800 = **~EUR 2,840 m** (~EUR 2.84 bn)
- **2.5% of Prämien (gross written premiums):** 0.025 × 14,738 = **~EUR 368 m**
- **Operative § 95c trigger threshold (the lower of the two materially relevant references):** **~EUR 368 m** (2.5% premium benchmark) — well above any single disclosed RPT.

(Note: § 95c AktG references the "letzten festgestellten Jahresabschluss" — i.e. consolidated total assets only; the 2.5% premium benchmark is used here as an additional insurer-specific sanity check requested in the analysis brief.)

## Threshold breach?
**N (No).** No individual related-party transaction disclosed in the FY2024 Group Annual Report exceeds the § 95c 5% Bilanzsumme threshold (EUR ~2.84 bn) or the 2.5% premium benchmark (EUR ~368 m). VIG's parallel SFCR explicitly states that "no very significant internal Group transactions exceeding the [5% of SCR] threshold were performed during the 2024 reporting period," consistent with the IFRS picture.

## Conclusion (Schwellenwertanalyse)
For FY2024 no VIG related-party transaction reaches the § 95c AktG materiality threshold (5% Bilanzsumme = EUR ~2,840 m; insurer 2.5%-premium benchmark = EUR ~368 m). The largest single disclosed RPT is the EUR 150 m intragroup guarantee for VIG HU GmbH, ~5.3% of the 5% BS threshold and ~41% of the 2.5% premium benchmark. No § 95c-Bekanntmachungen were published by VIG in 2024.
