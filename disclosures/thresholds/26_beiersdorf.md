# Beiersdorf AG — SRD II / § 111b–c AktG Threshold Analysis FY2024

**Index:** DAX · **Country:** Germany · **Period:** FY2024
**Legal basis:** §§ 111a–c AktG (ARUG II, in force since 1 January 2020); materiality threshold per § 111b Abs. 1 AktG = **1.5 % of the sum of non-current and current assets (Konzernbilanzsumme)** of the listed company according to the most recently adopted consolidated financial statements.

## 1. Reference balance sheet (FY2024)
- **Consolidated total assets (Konzernbilanzsumme) 31.12.2024:** **€13,011 m**
  - Source: Beiersdorf Annual Report 2024 — Consolidated Balance Sheet
    (https://reports.beiersdorf.com/annual-report/2024/consolidated-financial-statements/consolidated-financial-statements/balance-sheet.html)
- For reference, single-entity Beiersdorf AG (HGB) Bilanzsumme: €7,713 m (PY €7,700 m). The § 111b threshold is computed on the **Konzernbilanzsumme** of the listed company.

## 2. Materiality threshold (DE: § 111b Abs. 1 AktG)
- 1.5 % × €13,011 m = **€195.165 m** per single transaction with the same related party in the financial year (aggregation rule of § 111b Abs. 1 S. 2 AktG applies for transactions within 12 months with the same related party).

- Single largest individual RPT: **tesa Business Segment purchases of goods from associate Alkynes Co. Ltd., South Korea ≈ €13.4 m** (PY €11.7 m).
- Other notable items:
  - IT services Beiersdorf Shared Services GmbH → TCHIBO GmbH ≈ €3.3 m
  - IT services → Tchibo Coffee International Ltd. ≈ €0.4 m
  - Beiersdorf Hautpflege GmbH (NIVEA Haus operator) remuneration ≈ €3.3 m
- Aggregate disclosed quantified RPT volume (excl. KMP) ≈ **€20.4 m** for FY2024.
- KMP compensation (Executive Board IFRS total expense): €23.609 m — excluded from § 111b scope (§ 111a Abs. 3 Nr. 4 AktG).
- Loans to/from related parties: none disclosed.

## 4. Threshold test
- Largest single RPT €13.4 m vs. threshold €195.165 m → **0.10 % of total assets** (well below 1.5 %).
- Aggregate quantified RPT volume €20.4 m → **0.16 % of total assets** (also well below).
- → **No § 111b/§ 111c-relevant transaction in FY2024.**
