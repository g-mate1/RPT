## 2. DE statutory threshold under § 111b (1) AktG
- **Threshold = 1.5% × Konzern-Bilanzsumme = 1.5% × EUR ~72.7 bn ≈ EUR 1.09 bn**
- A single related-party transaction must reach > ~EUR 1.1 bn (cumulative within FY) to trigger Aufsichtsrats-Zustimmung and § 111c-Bekanntmachung.

## 3. Largest disclosed RPT (FY2024)
- **KfW (Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau)** — public-law institution wholly owned by the Federal Republic of Germany — is the largest shareholder of Deutsche Post AG (16.99% as of 31 Dec 2024; previous year 20.49%). On 6 Feb 2024 KfW sold 50 million shares; the capital reduction in May 2024 then raised KfW's stake to 16.99%.
- Largest economic RPT-flow: **KfW dividend** on its stake. With FY2024 dividend EUR 1.85 per share (proposed for AGM May 2025) × ~206 m shares held by KfW (16.99% × ~1.213 bn shares) ≈ **EUR ~380 m** (paid 2025 in respect of FY2024); well below threshold.
- Net income from investments accounted for using the equity method: **EUR 33 m** (FY2024; −EUR 128 m YoY) — transactions with associates/JVs (Locker Italia S.p.A.; CTT Expresso JV in Spain/Portugal) at arm's length, immaterial relative to threshold.
- KMP compensation (Vorstand + Aufsichtsrat) per Remuneration Report 2024: low double-digit EUR m — well below threshold.
- Government-related entity exemption (IAS 24.25–27) applied to other Federal-Republic-controlled entities for individually insignificant transactions on standard market terms.

## 4. Threshold breach? — **N (No)**
- No FY2024 RPT exceeds 1.5% of consolidated Bilanzsumme (~EUR 1.09 bn).
- KfW dividend (~EUR 0.38 bn) is the largest related-party cash flow but ~1/3 of the threshold.
- Consequently no § 111b Aufsichtsrats-Zustimmung and no § 111c-Bekanntmachung is triggered for FY2024.
