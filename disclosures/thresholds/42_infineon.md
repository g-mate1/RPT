## 1. Balance Sheet Total (FY2024)
- **Total assets (Bilanzsumme): ~EUR 27,978 million** (FY2024, 30 Sept 2024 consolidated IFRS BS)
  Source: Infineon Annual Report 2024, Consolidated Statement of Financial Position.

## 2. DE Threshold (§ 111b AktG — SRD II)
- Threshold = **1.5% × Bilanzsumme**
- 1.5% × EUR 27,978m = **~EUR 419.7 million**

## 3. Largest disclosed Related Party Transaction
- **ESMC GmbH (Dresden) JV equity contribution commitment: ~EUR 500 million** (Infineon's 10% stake in TSMC/Bosch/Infineon/NXP joint venture, over fab build-out phase, multi-year).
- Annual FY2024 share of profit from equity-method investees: EUR 11m (immaterial).
- All other RPT flows (sales/purchases/receivables/payables with non-consolidated subs, JVs, associates, KMP): immaterial / not separately disclosed.

## 4. Threshold Breach? (Y/N)
- **N** — although the ESMC equity commitment (~EUR 500m) nominally exceeds the EUR 419.7m threshold over the full multi-year build-out, Infineon's Corporate Governance Statement 2024 expressly states: *"There were no related party transactions requiring approval in the 2024 fiscal year."*
- Rationale: (i) the ESMC capital contribution is a JV capital injection treated under equity-method investment accounting, falling under the ordinary-course / market-conditions exemption of § 111a (2) AktG; (ii) it was committed/structured in FY2023 at JV formation; (iii) the FY2024 tranche drawdowns individually did not exceed the threshold; and (iv) Infineon's IFAC committee determined no approval-requiring RPT under § 111b AktG.
