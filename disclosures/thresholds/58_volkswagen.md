## 1. Balance Sheet Total (FY2024)
- **Total assets (Bilanzsumme, IFRS consolidated, Volkswagen Group): ~EUR 624,000 million** (31 Dec 2024)
  - Prior year: ~EUR 600,300 m (31 Dec 2023). Source: Volkswagen Group Annual Report 2024, Consolidated Statement of Financial Position. The figure aggregates the Automotive Division (passenger cars, commercial vehicles, power engineering) and the Financial Services Division (which alone holds ~EUR 260bn of receivables and lease assets and dominates current/non-current financial assets).

## 2. DE Threshold (§ 111b AktG — SRD II)
- Threshold = **1.5% × Bilanzsumme**
- 1.5% × EUR 624,000 m = **~EUR 9,360 million**
- VW Group itself confirms the threshold methodology verbatim in the Notes: *"All transactions with the individual related parties are analyzed to determine whether they exceed the threshold of 1.5% of the Volkswagen Group's total fixed and current assets, above which obligations under the rules on related party transactions apply (RPT threshold)."*

- **Loans receivable from joint ventures (predominantly Chinese JVs FAW-Volkswagen, SAIC Volkswagen, JAC-Volkswagen, plus Volkswagen Anhui / CARIAD-China JV): EUR 11,941 m** (PY EUR 12,068 m) — single largest disclosed RPT balance

- Loans receivable from non-consolidated subsidiaries: EUR 2,920 m (PY 1,266 m) — incl. CARIAD-China set-up loans and various non-consolidated entities
- Dividends received from JVs/associates (net of WHT): EUR 2,614 m (PY 2,450 m) — Chinese JV profit distributions
- Dividend paid to Porsche SE: EUR 1,703 m (PY 1,529 m)

**Largest single disclosed RPT item:** Loans to joint ventures **EUR 11,941 m** (exceeds threshold of EUR 9,360 m on a gross basis).

## 4. Threshold Breach? (Y/N)
- **N** — Despite the gross JV-loan balance (EUR 11,941 m) nominally exceeding the EUR 9.36bn threshold, Volkswagen AG published **no § 111c AktG notice in FY2024**. Rationale per § 111a AktG carve-outs:
  (i) **Konzernprivileg (§ 111a (3) Nr. 1 AktG)** does **not** apply to non-consolidated subsidiaries or joint ventures (FAW-VW, SAIC-VW etc. are equity-method JVs, not fully consolidated subsidiaries), so this carve-out is unavailable for the JV loans.
  (ii) **Ordinary course & arm's length (§ 111a (2) AktG)**: VW Group expressly states *"All transactions with related parties are regularly conducted on an arm's length basis"*; the Audit Committee of the Supervisory Board reviews RPTs against the 1.5% threshold and determined them to be ordinary-course business under group transfer-pricing rules. The JV loans, trade flows and dividend distributions to/from Chinese JVs are recurring, arm's length, ordinary-course financing/supply transactions and therefore exempt from the § 111b approval and § 111c publication requirement.
  (iii) **Dividends to Porsche SE and Lower Saxony** are pro-rata to all ordinary shareholders → § 111a (2) Satz 2 Nr. 2 AktG exemption (Maßnahmen, die allen Aktionären zu gleichen Bedingungen angeboten werden).
  (iv) Aggregation analysis is performed transaction-by-transaction per individual related party; no individual non-ordinary-course transaction with a single counterparty exceeded the threshold without falling under a carve-out in FY2024.

- **Number of public § 111c notices on volkswagen-group.com in FY2024: 0 (zero)**

- No notices titled "Bekanntmachung gemäß § 111c AktG" / "Geschäft mit nahestehenden Personen" were published in FY2024.
- Confirmed by absence in the 2024 Erklärung zur Unternehmensführung and by the IAS 24 note in the 2024 Annual Report (no approval-requiring transactions). Despite VW being one of the most monitored DAX issuers with the largest related-party population in Germany (Porsche SE, Lower Saxony, Qatar Holding, Porsche AG sub-group, dozens of Chinese JVs, CARIAD JVs, PowerCo associates), the consistent application of the ordinary-course / arm's length carve-out under § 111a (2) AktG and the dividend carve-out has resulted in **zero § 111c notices in FY2024** (and indeed since the introduction of the ARUG II regime in 2020).
