## Größter offengelegter RPT FY2024

- **KTM AG financial receivable from PIERER New Mobility GmbH (sub-group): EUR 372.0 m as at 31 Dec 2024** (arising from transfer of the new-mobility products business from KTM Group to PIERER Mobility AG with effect from 1 June 2024).
- This single related-party position (EUR 372.0 m) materially **exceeds** the § 95c-Schwelle of ~ EUR 46.8 m (factor ~ 8x).

Other material RPTs FY2024:
- MV Agusta Motor S.p.A. call option exercise (March 2024): purchase price EUR 45 m + min. EUR 5 m earn-out — borderline / below 5% BS but close to 2.5% U threshold.
- PB Invest GmbH / Vöcklabrucker Metallgießerei acquisition from Pierer Industrie AG (Sept 2024) — purchase price not separately disclosed but below threshold based on entity size.
- PIERER Green Energy GmbH sale to Pierer Industrie AG (June 2024): EUR 0.3 m — below threshold.
- Bajaj Auto Ltd. funding for KTM restructuring quotas — subsequent event (2025), not FY2024.

## Schwelle überschritten? **YES (Y)**

The EUR 372.0 m KTM AG ↔ PIERER New Mobility GmbH receivable, and the related intragroup transfer of the new-mobility business from KTM to PIERER Mobility AG (with effect from 1 June 2024), is a related-party transaction that exceeds the § 95c-Schwelle of ~ EUR 46.8 m.
