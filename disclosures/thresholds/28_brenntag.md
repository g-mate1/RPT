## 4. Y/N — Material RPT above § 111b/111c threshold?

**N (No).**
The largest reported transaction (~EUR 12–14 m KMP compensation) is well below the EUR 175 m threshold. Furthermore, KMP compensation that is part of the ordinary remuneration framework approved under §§ 87a, 113, 120a AktG is exempt from § 111b/111c by virtue of § 111a Abs. 3 Nr. 1 AktG. All other RPTs (associates / JVs / non-consolidated subsidiaries) are explicitly disclosed as **immaterial** and in the **ordinary course of business at arm's length**, which also qualifies them for the § 111a Abs. 2 AktG ordinary-course exemption.
