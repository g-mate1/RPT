## 4. Threshold Breach? (Y/N)
- **N** — Porsche AG's Corporate Governance Statement / Erklärung zur Unternehmensführung 2024 confirms that **no related party transactions required approval under § 111b AktG in FY2024**.
- Rationale (§ 111a (2) AktG carve-outs apply):
  (i) **Konzernprivileg / group-internal exemption (§ 111a (3) Nr. 1 AktG)**: Volkswagen Group cash-pooling, intra-group loans, trade flows and profit-and-loss transfer arrangements are between Porsche AG and 100%-controlled subsidiaries within the meaning of consolidation or with Volkswagen Group companies that fall under the German group-privilege exemption (or are governed by the VW Group transfer pricing guideline at arm's length and fall under the ordinary-course exemption).
  (ii) **Ordinary course & arm's length (§ 111a (2) AktG)**: all supply, R&D, IT and treasury arrangements with Volkswagen Group are explicitly stated to be at arm's length under standardised group transfer-pricing rules.
  (iii) **Dividends** to Porsche Holding Stuttgart GmbH and Porsche SE are pro-rata distributions on ordinary shares and are exempt from § 111b AktG (§ 111a (2) Satz 2 Nr. 2 AktG — Maßnahmen, die allen Aktionären zu gleichen Bedingungen angeboten werden).
  (iv) The Audit Committee of the Porsche AG Supervisory Board reviewed RPTs and determined no approval-requiring transaction under § 111b AktG in FY2024.
