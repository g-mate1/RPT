## FY2024 Reference Figures (EUR mn)
- **Bilanzsumme (BS, Total Assets, 31.12.2024):** ~ EUR 45,517 mn (consolidated; per OMV Combined Annual Report 2024 / cross-reference Investing.com)

## Austrian Thresholds (§ 95a AktG / OMV practice)
- **Supervisory Board approval (Material RPT):** > 5% of BS → ~ EUR 2,276 mn
- **Public disclosure (Bekanntmachung):** > 10% of BS → ~ EUR 4,552 mn
- **Alternative U threshold (2.5%):** ~ EUR 850 mn
- Note: Austrian law uses asset-based 5%/10% test (BS); 2.5% U is comparative reference only.

## Largest disclosed RPT (per Related Parties note FY2024)
- **Borealis AG guarantee for Borouge 4 LLC (Italian ECA funding):** EUR 1,228 mn total guarantee (drawn portion EUR 1,009 mn)
- Bayport Polymers member loan: EUR 769 mn
- Borouge 4 shareholder loan commitment: EUR 1,028 mn (EUR 435 mn drawn)
- **Largest single item:** EUR 1,228 mn (Borouge 4 ECA guarantee)

## Threshold breach assessment
- Largest RPT EUR 1,228 mn vs. 5% BS threshold EUR 2,276 mn → **below 5%**
- vs. 10% BS public-disclosure threshold EUR 4,552 mn → **below 10%**
- **Bekanntmachung gemäß § 95a Abs 5 AktG required in 2024:** **N (No)** — no individual RPT exceeded 10% BS threshold in FY2024
