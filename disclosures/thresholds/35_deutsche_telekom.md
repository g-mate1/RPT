## 2. DE Schwellenwert nach § 111b Abs. 1 AktG

- **Schwelle = 1,5 % der Bilanzsumme** (Summe aus Anlage- und Umlaufvermögen des letzten festgestellten Jahresabschlusses, Konzernbasis bei kapitalmarktorientierten Mutterunternehmen)
- 1,5 % × EUR 304,900m = **EUR 4,573.5 million**
- Aggregation rule: 12-month rolling aggregation of transactions with the same related party (§ 111b Abs. 2 AktG); approval and § 111c publication trigger only if a single transaction OR the rolling 12-month aggregate exceeds the threshold

Important caveats reducing the publication trigger:
- The GD tower lease liability is the **carrying amount at year-end** of a sale-and-leaseback originating from the **51 % stake sale in February 2023** (already disclosed before 12-month rolling window). The 2024 movement is only the period-on-period change (EUR 4,600m vs. prior year EUR 4,800m = a *decrease* of EUR 200m), so no new "transaction" with a value above EUR 4,573.5m took place during 2024.
- New shareholder loans during 2024 are well below threshold (net change EUR -233m at GD tower; nil at Glasfaser NordWest).
- All routine commercial transactions with the Federal Republic of Germany (27.8 % shareholder) qualify for the **IAS 24.26 / § 111a Abs. 2 AktG "ordinary course of business, market terms" exemption** and are explicitly not subject to § 111c publication.

## 6. Assessment

Deutsche Telekom — despite a balance-sheet item (GD tower lease liability) numerically slightly above the 1.5 % threshold — did **not** publish any § 111c notice in 2024. The reasons are consistent with German market practice and the wording of § 111a–111c AktG:

1. The GD tower lease liability **originates from the 2023 sale-and-leaseback transaction**, which was completed and disclosed at that time; the 2024 carrying amount is the residual obligation, not a new approval-requiring transaction.
2. **No incremental related-party transaction in 2024** breached the EUR 4,573.5m threshold either individually or on a 12-month aggregated basis with the same counterparty.
3. The Federal Republic of Germany's (27.8 %) ordinary-course customer/supplier relationships qualify for the **arm's-length / ordinary-course exemption** under § 111a Abs. 2 AktG.
4. The user's hypothesis that "Deutsche Telekom is one of the few DAX issuers known to publish § 111c notices on a dedicated page" is **not supported by the evidence**: no such dedicated page or 2024 publication exists. (Among DAX members, dedicated § 111c pages with actual 2024 entries have been documented at e.g. **Munich Re** — see https://www.munichre.com/en/company/investors/mandatory-announcements/material-transactions-with-related-parties.html — but not at Deutsche Telekom.)
