## 2) Austrian thresholds (AktG § 95a / VO-Anlage)

Austria implemented SRD II via the AktRÄG 2019. Material RPTs are defined by reference to § 95a AktG:
- **Approval threshold (Aufsichtsrat):** 5% of consolidated balance-sheet total (Konzernbilanzsumme).
- **Public-announcement threshold (Bekanntmachung, § 95c):** 10% of consolidated balance-sheet total (or, alternatively, transactions affecting >2.5% of consolidated revenue per some readings of the law — the 10% BS test is the principal trigger under Austrian law).

For comparability with the user's request, applying **5% BS and 2.5% Umsatz**:

| Threshold | Basis | Value (EUR m) |
|---|---|---|
| 5% of Bilanzsumme | 6,500 × 5% | **325.0** |
| 2.5% of Umsatz | 3,123.1 × 2.5% | **78.1** |
| 10% of Bilanzsumme (§ 95c stat. trigger) | 6,500 × 10% | 650.0 |

Lower threshold (more conservative materiality) ≈ **EUR 78.1m**.

## 4) Threshold breach?

- EUR 65.3m (ÖBAG dividend) < EUR 78.1m (2.5% U) → **No** breach of 2.5% Umsatz threshold.
- EUR 65.3m < EUR 325.0m (5% BS) → No breach.
- EUR 65.3m < EUR 650m (statutory § 95c 10% BS) → No § 95c Bekanntmachung required.

**Answer: N (no threshold breached by any individually disclosed RPT).**

Dividends to shareholders are also generally outside the RPT regime per § 95a Abs 5 AktG (transactions in the ordinary course / on market terms / pro-rata shareholder distributions exemption).
