- Ongoing arm's-length trademark licence, IT and shared-services agreements with Siemens Energy AG and Siemens Healthineers AG (Healthineers fully consolidated at ~75%, so eliminated).

- Pension contributions to Siemens Pension-Trust e.V. (multi-hundred-million EUR) — separate pensions note.
- KMP / Managing Board total compensation ~EUR 50–80 m; Supervisory Board ~EUR 4–5 m.
- **No single RPT at or above EUR ~2.22 bn** — largest individual exposure (associate sales / pension-trust contributions) materially below threshold.
