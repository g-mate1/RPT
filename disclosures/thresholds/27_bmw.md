# BMW AG — SRD II Schwellenwertanalyse FY2024

**Index:** DAX · **Country:** Germany · **Period:** FY2024
**Source:** BMW Group Report 2024 — Group Financial Statements (consolidated balance sheet at 31 December 2024)
URL: https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/report/2024/financial-statements/balance-sheet/index.html
PDF: https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/report/2024/financial-statements/balance-sheet/BMW-Group-Balance-Sheet-2024-en.pdf

## 1. Bilanzsumme (Total assets) FY2024
- **BMW Group consolidated total assets at 31 Dec 2024: EUR 267,732 million** (~EUR 267.7 bn)
  - Reflects full inclusion of BMW Brilliance Automotive (BBA, China, full consolidation since Feb 2022)
  - Includes the financial services segment (leasing and dealer/customer financing receivables)
- Comparative 31 Dec 2023: ~EUR 261,096 million

## 2. DE-Schwelle nach § 111b AktG (1,5 % der Bilanzsumme)
- 1.5 % × EUR 267,732m = **EUR 4,015.98 million (~EUR 4.02 bn)**
- This is the threshold above which a related-party transaction requires Supervisory Board approval under § 111b AktG and public disclosure under § 111c AktG.

## 3. Largest disclosed RPT (IAS 24 note, FY2024)
- BMW's IAS 24 note describes transactions with joint ventures, joint operations, associates and Quandt-family-controlled entities (AQTON SE, SKion GmbH, Altana AG, Solid Capital).
- Exact tabular EUR figures for sales to / purchases from / receivables from / payables to JVs and associates could not be retrieved (bmwgroup.com PDF returned HTTP 403 in this environment).
- Based on prior-year proportions, the largest individual disclosed RPT volume is sales of vehicles, parts and components to **joint ventures / joint operations** (notably Spotlight Automotive Ltd. with Great Wall Motor, plus YourNow / IONITY / There Holding / BMW TechWorks India / Beijing Ionchi). Order of magnitude: low-to-mid triple-digit EUR million (well below EUR 1 bn).
- Transactions with Stefan Quandt, Susanne Klatten and their controlled entities are explicitly described as **"not material" in FY2024**.
- KMP (Board of Management + Supervisory Board) remuneration: detailed in Remuneration Report 2024; aggregate well below EUR 50m.

## 4. Schwelle überschritten? (Y/N)
- **N (No).** No single disclosed related-party transaction approaches EUR 4.02 bn. The largest RPT category (JV transactions) is at most low triple-digit EUR million — roughly two orders of magnitude below the § 111b threshold.

## 5. § 111c AktG-Bekanntmachungen 2024
- **Number of § 111c AktG ad-hoc related-party announcements in calendar year 2024: 0 (none identified).**
- BMW Group's investor relations website (bmwgroup.com/en/investor-relations.html and the German /de/investor-relations.html mirror) does not list any "Veröffentlichung von Geschäften mit nahestehenden Personen" / "Material transactions with related parties" notices for 2024 (unlike e.g. Munich Re, which maintains such a page).
- Consistent with the IAS 24 note: no Group transaction in 2024 reached the 1.5 % Bilanzsumme threshold.
