## 4. Y/N – § 111c-Veröffentlichung erforderlich gewesen?
**N (no 2024 § 111c notice published by BASF SE).**

Rationale / why no notice despite EUR 2,943m JV purchase obligations:
- The EUR 2,943m figure is a **stock of long-term purchase obligations** (off-balance-sheet executory commitments under long-term PPAs), **not a single new RPT entered into in FY2024**. § 111c AktG applies to **new transactions (Geschäfte)** exceeding 1.5% of BS in a 12-month period, not to the carrying balance of pre-existing multi-year contracts.
- Under § 111a Abs. 2 AktG, transactions concluded **in the ordinary course of business and at arm's length** are exempt — BASF expressly states that "Transactions with related parties are carried out under normal market conditions" (Note 30), and BASF has a documented internal procedure (Aufsichtsrats-Geschäftsordnung) for verifying arm's-length character of RPTs in ordinary course.
- Intra-group transactions with **wholly-owned (consolidated) subsidiaries** are exempt under § 111a Abs. 3 Nr. 1 AktG.
- Pensionskasse VVaG financing (EUR 266m) is well below the threshold.
