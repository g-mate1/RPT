**Index:** DAX · **Country:** Germany · **Period:** FY2024

**IR page consulted:** https://www.bayer.com/en/investors/investors
**Regulatory base:** §§ 111a–111c AktG (ARUG II implementing SRD II); German threshold = 1.5 % × Bilanzsumme (consolidated total assets) of the latest approved annual financial statements.

## 1. Balance sheet (FY2024)
- Consolidated total assets (Bilanzsumme), Bayer Group, 31 Dec 2024: **EUR ~110,850 m** (≈ EUR 110.85 bn).
  - Source: Bayer Annual Report 2024, consolidated statement of financial position (direct PDF retrieval blocked from sandbox; figure cross-checked via Yahoo Finance balance-sheet feed of BAYN). Bayer AG single-entity balance sheet (HGB) Bilanzsumme is materially smaller, but §111b AktG references the konzernweite Bilanzsumme of the most recently approved consolidated accounts.

## 2. DE-Schwellenwert (§111b AktG)
- 1.5 % × EUR 110,850 m = **EUR ~1,662.75 m** (≈ EUR 1.66 bn).
- This is the materiality threshold above which a single related-party transaction (or aggregated transactions with the same related party within the rolling 12-month period) triggers Audit-Committee approval and a §111c AktG public notice.
