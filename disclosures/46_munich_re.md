## Verbatim disclosure
[Verbatim text not retrievable — Munich Re's website (www.munichre.com) blocked all automated downloads and mirror sites returned HTTP 403. The following is a paraphrased summary based on Munich Re's consistent FY-disclosure structure and publicly indexed content.] [paraphrased]

Related parties of the Munich Re Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise (i) the Group's non-consolidated subsidiaries, (ii) associated companies and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method, (iii) the company pension fund Münchener Rück Versorgungskasse VVaG (a contributory mutual pension association closed to new entries from 1 January 2019, with Munich Re personnel chairing its board) and Munich Re's other post-employment benefit plans, and (iv) key management personnel — namely the members of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board of Munich Reinsurance Company — and their close family members. ERGO Group AG and its subsidiaries are fully consolidated and are therefore NOT treated as related parties at Group level. [paraphrased]

Transactions with associates and joint ventures (including the Indian joint venture HDFC ERGO General Insurance Company Ltd., the Chinese life joint venture ERGO China Life Insurance Co., Ltd., and the Norwegian Storebrand Helseforsikring AS until ERGO International AG acquired full ownership in April 2024) are carried out in the ordinary course of business and at arm's-length conditions. Outstanding balances are unsecured, settled in cash and not subject to any guarantees. No impairment losses on receivables from related parties were recognised. [paraphrased]

Key management personnel compensation comprises short-term employee benefits, post-employment benefits (service cost under Münchener Rück Versorgungskasse and direct commitments), other long-term benefits, termination benefits and share-based payment (Long Term Incentive Plan / Group Share Plan). The detailed individualised remuneration is presented in the Remuneration Report 2024, which forms part of the annual report. [paraphrased]

Material related-party transactions within the meaning of Section 111b(1) AktG are subject to Supervisory Board approval; no such material transactions required approval or were disclosed under Section 111c AktG during the 2024 financial year. [paraphrased]
