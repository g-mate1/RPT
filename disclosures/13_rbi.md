[paraphrased] RBI discloses related-party relationships and transactions in accordance with IAS 24. The disclosed categories are: (i) the parent / ultimate controlling shareholders, being the Raiffeisen-Landesbanken (RLBs) of the Austrian Raiffeisen sector who together — primarily through Raiffeisen-Landesbanken-Holding GmbH (RLBHO) and the regional Raiffeisen-Landesbanken — control RBI AG; (ii) non-consolidated subsidiaries; (iii) joint ventures and associated companies accounted for at equity (including entities of the Raiffeisen sector such as Raiffeisen Bausparkasse, Raiffeisen Kapitalanlage, Raiffeisen Versicherung interests, and other equity-method investees); and (iv) key management personnel of RBI AG (Management Board and Supervisory Board) and their close family members.

[paraphrased] For each category RBI presents balance-sheet positions at year-end (loans and advances, debt securities, derivative financial instruments, deposits, debt securities issued, subordinated capital, other liabilities) and income-statement effects (interest income/expense, fee and commission income/expense, net trading result, other operating result). Off-balance-sheet items (guarantees granted and received, irrevocable credit commitments) are also disclosed.

[paraphrased] Transactions with the Russian subsidiary AO Raiffeisenbank are eliminated on consolidation (AO Raiffeisenbank is a fully-consolidated subsidiary) and therefore are not shown as related-party transactions in the consolidated note; however, RBI describes the situation around AO Raiffeisenbank — including sanctions, the Russian court order in the Rasperia/STRABAG case requiring AO Raiffeisenbank to pay EUR ~2,044 million in damages and to acquire Rasperia's STRABAG shares (booked as a provision/loss in 2024), and the share-transfer ban imposed by Russian authorities — in separate notes on Russia exposure, legal risk, and significant subsequent events / contingent liabilities.

[paraphrased] Key management personnel compensation (Management Board + Supervisory Board of RBI AG) is disclosed in aggregate by category in line with IAS 24.17: short-term employee benefits (fixed and variable cash remuneration), post-employment benefits (pension contributions), other long-term benefits, termination benefits and share-based / instrument-based payments. Detailed figures by individual board member are set out in the separately published Remuneration Report 2024 of RBI AG.

[paraphrased] The note states that all transactions with related parties were conducted on an arm's-length basis under conditions equivalent to those for arm's-length transactions / customary banking and market terms.
