[paraphrased] Shareholder structure and controlling parties. As of 31 December 2024, América Móvil B.V. (Netherlands), a wholly owned subsidiary of América Móvil, S.A.B. de C.V., Mexico, holds 60.6% of the shares of Telekom Austria AG. The Republic of Austria, via Österreichische Beteiligungs AG (ÖBAG), holds 28.4%. The remaining 11.0% is free float. A shareholders' agreement between ÖBAG and América Móvil (entered into force 27 June 2014; extended on 6 February 2023 for a further ten years) commits the parties to a joint long-term policy regarding the management of Telekom Austria. The Supervisory Board has ten shareholder-elected members, eight nominated by América Móvil (including the Deputy Chair) and two nominated by ÖBAG (including the Chair); the right to nominate the CEO lies with América Móvil. The ultimate parent company of the A1 Group is América Móvil, S.A.B. de C.V.

[paraphrased] Related parties of the A1 Group therefore include: (i) the ultimate parent América Móvil, S.A.B. de C.V. and its other subsidiaries and affiliates outside the A1 Group (the "América Móvil group"); (ii) ÖBAG and other entities controlled or significantly influenced by the Republic of Austria (state-related entities such as ÖBB, Verbund AG, Österreichische Post AG, OMV AG and other federal-government-controlled corporates with which A1 Group enters into telecommunications service contracts on an arm's-length basis); (iii) the EuroTeleSites Group, which was spun off on 22 September 2023 and to which the A1 Group is the anchor tenant under local Master Lease Agreements; (iv) associated companies and joint ventures of the A1 Group; and (v) key management personnel (Management Board and Supervisory Board members of Telekom Austria AG).

[paraphrased] Transactions with the EuroTeleSites Group (anchor-tenant master lease arrangements). Following the demerger of EuroTeleSites AG on 22 September 2023, the A1 Group's operating subsidiaries entered into local Master Lease Agreements with the corresponding EuroTeleSites operating companies for the use of passive tower infrastructure. The agreements are indefinite in term, with termination options. Rent and other price elements are adjusted annually by 85% of the annual local CPI change, capped at 3% per year. EuroTeleSites' FY2024 group revenue of mEUR 270.2 (vs. mEUR 257 in FY2023) is described as being 85%–96% attributable to A1 Group per segment, and 90% across the EuroTeleSites group as a whole — implying that A1 Group's lease-related operating expenses paid to EuroTeleSites in FY2024 were of the order of mEUR 243 (90% × 270.2). [paraphrased estimate based on EuroTeleSites disclosure] In addition, on 1 April 2024 the rents under the master lease agreements were increased by 3% (the contractual cap) in all operating countries.

[paraphrased] Cash pooling and intra-group financial settlements with EuroTeleSites. Cash inflows in the A1 Group's investing activities related to the settlement of EuroTeleSites cash-pooling balances with the A1 Group are disclosed. Receivables of EuroTeleSites from A1 Towers Holding GmbH related to the VAT group amounted to tEUR 8,539 at 31 December 2024 (prior year: tEUR 0); receivables related to the "ETS-fee" (charging of centrally provided support services from EuroTeleSites to A1 Towers Holding) amounted to tEUR 3,580 (prior year: tEUR 1,348). These amounts represent the corresponding payables of the A1 Group to EuroTeleSites.

[paraphrased] Transactions with shareholders / dividend declared. The dividend declared by Telekom Austria AG for the financial year 2023 and approved at the AGM in June 2024 (EUR 0.36 per share) was paid in 2024; as of 30 June 2024 the declared dividend payable to the main shareholders América Móvil and ÖBAG resulted in an increase in payables due to related parties of EUR 211 million (this was the H1 2024 snapshot of the unpaid dividend obligation). For the financial year 2024 the Management Board proposes a dividend of EUR 0.40 per share to the AGM 2025; the proportion attributable to América Móvil (60.6%) and ÖBAG (28.4%) constitutes dividends to related parties.

[paraphrased] Transactions with the América Móvil group (other than dividends). The A1 Group reports transactions with other companies of the América Móvil group in the ordinary course of business — primarily international voice and data interconnection (wholesale carrier services), roaming traffic, content and platform-related cross-charges, and procurement-related cooperation. Such transactions are stated to be concluded on arm's-length terms. Specific FY2024 EUR amounts for sales to, purchases from, receivables from and payables to América Móvil group entities outside the A1 Group are reported in the related-party note as part of the line "transactions with other entities under common control / ultimate parent". [paraphrased] Transactions with state-controlled entities (ÖBAG-related). Because ÖBAG, an entity controlled by the Republic of Austria, has significant influence over the A1 Group, other entities controlled or significantly influenced by the Republic of Austria are related parties. The A1 Group has ordinary-course commercial relations with such state-related entities (telecommunications services to/from Austrian government bodies, ÖBB, Austrian Post, Verbund AG, OMV and similar). The Supervisory Board has approved contracts in connection with telecommunications services between A1 and members of the Supervisory Board and their companies, as well as contracts relating to energy supply and a sales partnership with Verbund AG, all on arm's-length terms. The A1 Group invokes the partial exemption in IAS 24.25 for government-related entities and discloses such transactions only where individually significant.

[paraphrased] Key management personnel (KMP) compensation. Total remuneration owed to the members of the Management Board of Telekom Austria AG in FY2024 amounted to approximately EUR 4.14 million (prior year: EUR 5.15 million, excluding termination components in connection with the departure of Siegfried Mayrhofer). Components: fixed/non-performance-related EUR 1.55 million (FY2023: EUR 1.86 million); short-term variable (STI) EUR 1.25 million (FY2023: EUR 1.90 million); long-term variable (LTI, expected payout) EUR 1.33 million. Supervisory Board remuneration and pension/post-employment-related amounts for KMP are disclosed separately in the Remuneration Report 2024 (https://a1.group/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/05/Remuneration-Report-2024.pdf).
