"All transactions with related parties concern the key management personnel of Zalando, carried out with members of the management board or supervisory board, their close family members, or with entities controlled or jointly controlled by those persons.

Merchandise of EUR 122.4m was ordered from these related parties in the reporting period (prior-year period: EUR 121.8m). Goods totalling EUR 0.7m were sold to related parties (prior-year period: EUR 1.7m), and services totalling EUR 15.0m were provided to related parties (prior-year period: EUR 13.1m). No services were received from related parties in the reporting period.

Transactions with entities controlled or jointly controlled by key management personnel resulting from the purchase of goods and services gave rise to liabilities of EUR 170.8m as of 31 December 2024 (prior year: EUR 140.3m).

Transactions with key management personnel themselves related only to the exercise of options under share-based payment awards granted to the members of the management board as part of management board remuneration. In the reporting period, members of the management board exercised 12,342 options under ZOP 2021 and 268,372 options under LTI.

[...] Supervisory board member Anders Holch Povlsen (CEO of Bestseller A/S, ~10% shareholder of Zalando) abstained from discussion and resolution regarding the creation of the joint venture FashionTrade between the Bestseller group and the Zalando group to avoid conflicts of interest. [...]

Detailed remuneration of the Management Board and the Supervisory Board is disclosed in the Remuneration Report. Maximum total remuneration is capped at EUR 15.75m per co-CEO and EUR 10.5m per other Management Board member per financial year."
