Ownership / control structure. Schoeller-Bleckmann Oilfield Equipment AG (renamed SBO AG in April 2025 following AGM approval) is a listed Austrian stock corporation (ATX prime, Vienna Stock Exchange; ISIN AT0000946652). As at 31 December 2024 the principal / anchor shareholder is Berndorf Industrieholding AG (BIHAG), Berndorf, Austria, holding approximately 33.6% of the share capital, with the remaining c. 66.4% in free float (mainly institutional investors from the US, UK and Norway, including BlackRock, Vanguard and Dimensional Fund Advisors). Berndorf Industrieholding AG is part of the Berndorf group, ultimately controlled by Berndorf Privatstiftung (founded by Norbert Zimmermann). SBO does not have a controlling or majority shareholder; BIHAG exercises significant influence and is treated as a related party under IAS 24. [paraphrased]

Identified related parties of the SBO Group in FY2024 included: (i) the principal shareholder Berndorf Industrieholding AG and other entities of the Berndorf group / Berndorf Privatstiftung (including Berndorf AG); (ii) members of the Executive Board (Vorstand) of SBO and their close family members — Klaus Mader (Chairman of the Executive Board / CEO from 1 January 2024; previously CFO), and the other Executive Board members serving during FY2024; (iii) members of the Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) of SBO and their close family members, including Norbert Zimmermann (Chairman of the Supervisory Board; also Chairman of the Board of Berndorf Privatstiftung), Sonja Zimmermann (Supervisory Board member; Chairwoman of the Supervisory Board of Berndorf AG and various other Berndorf-group positions), Franz Viehböck (newly elected to the SBO Supervisory Board; CEO of Berndorf AG), and the other Supervisory Board members; (iv) associates and joint ventures of the Group accounted for at equity, where applicable; (v) non-consolidated subsidiaries, where applicable. [paraphrased]

Transactions in the ordinary course of business. In FY2024 there were no individually material trading transactions (sales of goods / services, purchases of goods / services, financing transactions, guarantees, leases or comparable transactions) between SBO Group companies and Berndorf Industrieholding AG, Berndorf AG, Berndorf Privatstiftung or other Berndorf-group entities that would, under IAS 24, require disclosure of specific amounts. SBO and the Berndorf group operate in different industrial sectors (oilfield / energy equipment and high-precision components for SBO; diversified industrial holdings for Berndorf) and ordinary-course inter-group trading is limited. Where ad-hoc transactions occur, they are conducted at arm's length. Directors' Dealings during FY2024. On 27 May 2024 Berndorf Industrieholding AG (BIHAG), as a person closely associated with Supervisory Board member Sonja Zimmermann, acquired 30,000 SBO shares at EUR 50.00 per share off-venue (total volume EUR 1.5 million). This Directors' Dealings notification is published under the Market Abuse Regulation and is reflected in the EQS / ad-hoc disclosures referenced in the FY2024 Annual Financial Report. [paraphrased]

Dividends paid to related parties. SBO paid an ordinary dividend of EUR 2.00 per share for the FY2023 financial year (paid in May 2024) following AGM approval on 25 April 2024. Based on BIHAG's c. 33.6% interest, the dividend paid to BIHAG amounted to approximately EUR 5.3 million (EUR 2.00 x c. 16 million shares x 33.6%). For FY2024, the Executive Board proposed a dividend of EUR 1.75 per share (payout ratio 61%), payable after AGM approval in April 2025. [paraphrased — computed from public share-count and dividend disclosures]

Key Management Personnel (KMP) compensation. Total remuneration to the Executive Board and Supervisory Board of SBO is disclosed in the Notes and in the separate Remuneration Report (Vergütungsbericht 2024) submitted to and approved by the AGM in April 2025. The Executive Board during FY2024 comprised Klaus Mader (CEO from 1 January 2024, previously CFO), together with the other Executive Board members serving in FY2024 (CTO / COO and CFO functions following the 1 January 2024 reshuffle). Compensation consists of fixed remuneration, variable / performance-based remuneration linked to Group KPIs, post-employment / pension benefits, and other benefits in accordance with SBO's remuneration policy. Supervisory Board members receive fixed annual remuneration plus attendance fees in accordance with the AGM-approved remuneration policy. Specific aggregate amounts in EUR thousand for FY2024 are disclosed in the Remuneration Report 2024 / Notes but were not retrievable from the sandbox. [paraphrased]
