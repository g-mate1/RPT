Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG ("Porsche AG", domiciled in Stuttgart) defines related parties in accordance with IAS 24. Because Volkswagen AG indirectly holds approximately 75.4% of Porsche AG's share capital — corresponding to 75% minus one ordinary share — via the intermediate holding company Porsche Holding Stuttgart GmbH, Stuttgart, Porsche AG is included in the consolidated financial statements of Volkswagen AG, Wolfsburg, which prepares the consolidated financial statements for the largest group of companies. Porsche Automobil Holding SE ("Porsche SE"), Stuttgart, directly holds 25% plus one ordinary share of the ordinary shares of Porsche AG (≈12.5% of total share capital), and Porsche SE is the parent of the smallest group of companies into which Porsche AG is included. Porsche SE itself is controlled by the Porsche and Piëch families. As a result, the related parties of Porsche AG comprise Volkswagen AG and all of its consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates of the Volkswagen Group (the so-called "Volkswagen related companies"), Porsche SE and its subsidiaries, the Porsche/Piëch family members and entities they control, all non-consolidated subsidiaries of the Porsche AG Group, the joint ventures and associates of Porsche AG (including Bertrandt AG, Ehningen; Rimac Group d.o.o., Sveta Nedelja; and Bugatti Rimac d.o.o., Sveta Nedelja, all of which are material), and the members of the Executive Board and Supervisory Board of Porsche AG together with their close family members.

Significant ongoing relationships with Volkswagen Group companies cover purchasing and procurement cooperation (a "Purchasing and Procurement Cooperation Agreement" between Porsche AG and Volkswagen AG sets out general principles for continued cooperation), joint development of platforms and modular toolkits (MEB, PPE, SSP), shared production of certain vehicles (e.g. Porsche Cayenne / Volkswagen Touareg at Volkswagen Slovakia), IT services, treasury / cash-pooling arrangements with Volkswagen AG, intra-group financing, the supply of components and parts (engines, axles, electronics, batteries) from Volkswagen Group companies, and the sale of Porsche vehicles, parts and powertrains to Volkswagen Group companies. The Porsche Group tax guidelines and the Volkswagen AG Group transfer pricing guideline apply to the pricing of intercompany goods and services. Porsche Financial Services activities outside Germany are largely conducted through Volkswagen Group financial services entities. With Porsche SE, Porsche AG has ongoing relationships in connection with the IPO-related "Procedural and Amendment Agreement and Agreement to Amend the Comprehensive Agreement" concluded in 2022, which governs information rights and certain corporate-governance arrangements, and dividend payments on the ordinary shares held by Porsche SE.

[paraphrased] In fiscal year 2024 dividends of approximately EUR 1.7 bn (prior year: ≈ EUR 1.5 bn) were paid by Porsche AG to its shareholders, of which a material portion accrued to Porsche Holding Stuttgart GmbH (Volkswagen Group) on the ordinary shares it holds, and to Porsche SE on its ≈12.5% holding of ordinary shares; the remainder went to the free float of preference shares. At the parent-only (HGB) level, receivables of Porsche AG from affiliated companies (predominantly Volkswagen Group entities) at 31 December 2024 comprised: trade receivables EUR 2,370 m (PY: EUR 2,570 m); cash-pooling receivables EUR 2,062 m (PY: EUR 1,015 m); loans issued EUR 537 m (PY: EUR 382 m); and profit transfers EUR 254 m (PY: EUR 1,151 m). Liabilities to affiliated companies at the same date were EUR 1,366 m (PY: EUR 1,134 m), of which trade payables EUR 1,088 m (PY: EUR 1,055 m) and loss absorption obligations EUR 167 m (PY: EUR 2 m). A write-down of EUR 40 m was recognised on the loan receivable from Porsche Erste Beteiligungsgesellschaft mbH, Stuttgart.

[paraphrased] Key management personnel (KMP) of Porsche AG comprise the members of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board. Total remuneration disclosed in respect of fiscal year 2024 included a grant of 138,057 performance shares to the Executive Board with a fair value of approximately EUR 13 m at the grant date, plus fixed and short-term variable cash remuneration; total remuneration of the Supervisory Board including attendance fees amounted to EUR 3 m. Pensions for former members of the Executive Board and their surviving dependants, and the related defined-benefit obligations, are disclosed in the remuneration report.

Porsche AG states that all transactions with related parties are conducted at arm's length.
