Substance of the disclosure (consolidated): Siemens Energy AG identifies the following groups of related parties under IAS 24 as of 30 September 2024:
- Siemens AG (Munich) and its consolidated subsidiaries — although Siemens AG's shareholding had declined to 17.1% (after the December 2023 transfer of an 8.0% stake to Siemens Pension-Trust e.V.) and Siemens AG had ceased to account for Siemens Energy under the equity method, the Siemens AG group continues to be disclosed as a related party of Siemens Energy because of the still-significant shareholding combined with personal interlocks and the contractual relationships entered into at the time of the spin-off (services, trademark license, treasury, pensions, leases). [paraphrased]
- Siemens Pension-Trust e.V. and Siemens Pensionsfonds (which together hold a further substantial stake in Siemens Energy following the share transfers in June and December 2023). [paraphrased]
- Siemens Healthineers AG (a Siemens AG group company). [paraphrased]
- Siemens Stiftung (charitable foundation). [paraphrased]
- Joint ventures and associates of the Siemens Energy Group (transactions arising from ordinary operations with non-consolidated affiliates and equity-method investees). [paraphrased]
- Key management personnel (Executive Board and Supervisory Board members of Siemens Energy AG) and their close family members. [paraphrased]

Transactions with the Siemens AG group during FY2023/24 included ongoing supplies of goods and services (IT, real estate / leases of office space, treasury and cash management arrangements), the trademark license agreement (Siemens Energy pays Siemens AG a margin-based license fee ranging between 0.3% and 1.2% of revenue, excluding former Siemens Gamesa revenue, depending on Adjusted EBITA margin), pension-related arrangements with Siemens Pension-Trust / Pensionsfonds, and the settlement of legacy spin-off receivables/payables. [paraphrased]

In connection with the November 2023 stabilization package, Siemens Energy AG entered into counter-guarantee arrangements with the Federal Republic of Germany ("Bund") amounting to EUR 7.5 billion, which together with bank-issued guarantee lines (EUR 12 billion from a banking consortium) and approximately EUR 3 billion of further support form the overall guarantee package of about EUR 15 billion. The counter-guarantee from the Bund is disclosed as a transaction with a related party only to the extent the Bund qualifies under IAS 24; however, the disclosure of these counter-guarantees and the related restrictions (no dividend distribution, executive compensation restrictions, etc.) is included in the notes. Siemens Energy exited the federal counter-guarantee facility earlier than originally planned during fiscal 2024/2025. [paraphrased]

The most significant individual related-party transaction recorded in FY2023/24 was the sale, on 8 December 2023, of an 18% stake in Siemens Limited (India) to Siemens AG for a purchase price of EUR 2,081 million. This transaction generated a gain of approximately EUR 1,728 million recognized in "income from investments accounted for using the equity method, net". [paraphrased]

Key management personnel (KMP) compensation: Executive Board members during fiscal 2024 received exclusively fixed compensation (waiver of variable compensation as required under the Federal counter-guarantee agreement). Members included Christian Bruch (CEO), Maria Ferraro (CFO until early FY24) and her successor, Anne-Laure de Chammard, Vinod Philip, Karim Amin, and Tim Holt. Total Executive Board compensation for fiscal 2024 is reported in the Compensation Report (Annual Report 2024, p. 177 ff.) in EUR thousand line items per member. [paraphrased]
