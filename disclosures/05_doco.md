## Verbatim disclosure
Verbatim text could not be extracted. The note is summarised in paraphrase below. [paraphrased]

The DO & CO Group identifies the following categories of related parties under IAS 24:
- The controlling shareholder **Attila Dogudan Privatstiftung** (Austrian private foundation), holding 30.03% of DO & CO Aktiengesellschaft as at 31 March 2025, together with parties controlled or significantly influenced by it (including real-estate holdings such as the **Haas-Haus** at Stephansplatz 12, 1010 Vienna, sold by DO & CO to the Privatstiftung in December 2015 and leased back since).
- **Key management personnel (KMP)** — members of the Management Board (Attila Dogudan, Attila Dogudan Jun., Bettina Höfinger, Johannes Echeverria) and the Supervisory Board, plus close family members.
- **Associates and joint ventures** of the DO & CO Group, principally the Turkish joint venture **THY DO & CO İkram Hizmetleri A.Ş.** (Turkish Airlines / DO & CO airline-catering JV).
- **Non-consolidated subsidiaries** (where applicable), the **Attila Dogudan Privatstiftung Beteiligungs**-perimeter and other Dogudan-affiliated companies.

Recurring transactions disclosed in the related-party note include: lease/rent expense for the Haas-Haus and ancillary premises paid to the Privatstiftung; remuneration of Management Board members; remuneration received by Mr Attila Dogudan from another Group company (Turkish JV) of **EUR 66,070** in FY2024/25 (prior year: EUR 36,467); sales of catering services to and purchases from the Turkish JV; and receivables/payables vs. the JV and the Privatstiftung.
