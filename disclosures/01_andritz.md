[paraphrased] Key Management Personnel — figures disclosed in the FY2024 Remuneration Report:
- Fixed remuneration of all Executive Board members in FY2024: EUR 2,604,000 (FY2023: EUR 2,820,046).
- Total Supervisory Board remuneration FY2024: EUR 330,000 (FY2023: EUR 327,500).
- CEO mix: ~18% fixed / ~82% variable (STI + LTI). Other Executive Board members: ~17% fixed / ~83% variable on average.
- Minimum reported Group net income threshold for any STI payout for FY2024: EUR 115.76 million.

## Categories disclosed
- Transactions with associated companies (equity-method investees) — disclosed, characterised as not material; nature: deliveries and services.
- Transactions with non-consolidated subsidiaries — disclosed, characterised as not material; nature: deliveries and services.
- Key Management Personnel (KMP) compensation — disclosed in detail in separate Remuneration Report 2024 (Executive Board, Supervisory Board, fixed/variable, STI/LTI, pension, fringe benefits).
- D&O insurance for board members — disclosed.
- No disclosure of material loans to/from related parties.
- No disclosure of material guarantees to related parties.
- No disclosure of related-party real-estate, license/royalty, M&A, charitable foundation, or cash-pooling transactions of a material nature.
