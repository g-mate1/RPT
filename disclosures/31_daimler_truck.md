In accordance with IAS 24, Daimler Truck Holding AG ("Daimler Truck Group") discloses the following related party relationships:

**(a) Shareholders with significant influence.** Mercedes-Benz Group AG (Stuttgart) and Mercedes-Benz Pension Trust e.V. together held a significant interest in Daimler Truck Holding AG's voting share capital at year-end 2024. Mercedes-Benz Group AG's percentage of voting rights was approximately 36.38% as of 31 December 2024 (following share-capital reductions via redemption of treasury shares). Mercedes-Benz Pension Trust e.V. held approximately 5.19% (41.1 million shares per November 2024 voting-rights notification). Together with their affiliates, both shareholder entities are accounted for as related parties of the Daimler Truck Group.

**(b) Joint ventures and associated companies.** The principal joint ventures and associates considered to be related parties include: cellcentric GmbH & Co. KG (50/50 fuel-cell systems JV with the Volvo Group); Beijing Foton Daimler Automotive Co., Ltd. (BFDA, a 50/50 JV with Beijing Automotive Industry Holding/BAIC Foton — equity-investment carrying amount fully impaired in Q2 2024 for EUR −120 million); Milence B.V. (electric truck charging JV, equally held by Daimler Truck, the Volvo Group and the TRATON GROUP); the TBC dealer-network entities in North America (Tognum/MTU related); and other associates (e.g., Auman, certain dealership companies). Hino-Daimler Truck cooperation under the Toyota Motor Corporation/Hino umbrella was, during FY2024, still pre-completion and treated as a related party from the date of binding agreement to the extent applicable.

**(c) Transactions with Mercedes-Benz Group AG and its subsidiaries** continued under transitional and long-term cooperation/service agreements concluded in the context of the 2021 hive-down (IT services, R&D cooperation, joint procurement, components supply, real-estate use/leases on shared sites, group insurance, and selected administrative services). Sales of goods and services to, and purchases from, Mercedes-Benz Group entities are stated to be transacted on arm's-length terms. Joint and several liability of Mercedes-Benz Group AG, Mercedes-Benz AG and Daimler Truck AG persists for liabilities of Mercedes-Benz Group AG that arose before the hive-down took effect.

**(d) Key management personnel (KMP) compensation.** Remuneration of the members of the Board of Management and the Supervisory Board of Daimler Truck Holding AG is disclosed in line with IAS 24.17 and German Commercial Code requirements; details are presented in the separate Remuneration Report 2024 and reproduced in summary in this note (short-term benefits, share-based components/PSP, pension service cost). Payments to former Board members and their surviving dependants amounted to EUR 807 thousand in 2024.

**(e) Cash management / financial relationships.** Daimler Truck AG acts as the central in-house bank for the Group via cash-pooling agreements with subsidiaries; intra-group balances and interest are eliminated in consolidation and are therefore not reflected in related-party figures.
