Related parties of Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA in FY2024 comprise:

- **Else Kröner-Fresenius-Stiftung (EKFS)** — the largest shareholder of Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA, holding 151,842,509 ordinary shares (27.0% of subscribed capital at 31 December 2024); EKFS is also the sole shareholder of Fresenius Management SE, the General Partner of Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA.
- **Fresenius Management SE** — General Partner of the KGaA (no equity participation in the limited share capital); receives a statutory reimbursement of expenses incurred for managing the KGaA, including remuneration of its Management Board members (which is disclosed in the Compensation Report and forms part of the operating expenses of the Group).
- **Fresenius Medical Care AG** (FME) — equity-method investee in FY2024 following deconsolidation from end-2023; Fresenius SE holds approximately 32.2% of FME. The Group has ongoing arrangements with FME and its subsidiaries for services (e.g., trademark licence agreements, IT, real-estate leases, fixed-asset accounting, shared procurement) and for the sale and purchase of goods.
- **Fresenius Vamed entities** — During 2024 the Group initiated and substantially completed the structured exit from its Fresenius Vamed investment company. Following deconsolidation, the residual Vamed entities (Vamed AG; Vamed Gesundheit Holding Deutschland GmbH; the 30% minority retained in the rehabilitation business sold to PAI Partners; remaining Austrian operations to be sold to Porr/Strabag for €90 million) became related parties / equity-method investees.
- **Other equity-method investees** — including Vifor Fresenius Medical Care Renal Pharma Ltd (45% owned by FME) and other joint ventures and associates of the Helios, Kabi and Vamed segments.
- **Key management personnel (KMP)** — members of the Management Board and the Supervisory Board of Fresenius Management SE (acting as Management Board of Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA) and the Supervisory Board of Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA.

In FY2024, in connection with the Vamed exit, the Group granted loans to its now non-consolidated Vamed entities: **€452 million to Vamed Gesundheit Holding Deutschland GmbH** and **€76 million to Vamed AG**. The Vamed High-End Services (HES) business unit, which provides services for Fresenius Helios and other hospitals, was retained inside the Group (transferred to Fresenius).

Service, supply and lease relationships with related parties (including FME, Vifor FME Renal Pharma, and the deconsolidated Vamed entities) continued during the year on standard commercial terms. The Supervisory Board approves any individual transactions between the Company and EKFS that legally require its consent; in FY2024 no such transactions were carried out that required Supervisory Board approval.

Compensation of key management personnel (Management Board of the General Partner) for FY2024 includes cash compensation of €11,374 thousand (2023: €7,939 thousand), of which €5,748 thousand (2023: €7,939 thousand) was non-performance-based. Detailed individual remuneration, share-based and pension components are disclosed in the separately published Compensation Report 2024.

Transactions with related parties are concluded at arm's length / on customary market terms (paraphrased standard statement included by the Group's IFRS note).
