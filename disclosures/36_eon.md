[paraphrased] Note (33) "Transactions with Related Parties" in E.ON SE's Notes to the Consolidated Financial Statements for FY2024 discloses, in accordance with IAS 24, transactions between E.ON Group companies and the following categories of related parties:

- **RWE AG and the RWE Group** — RWE holds approximately 15% of E.ON SE's share capital following completion of the 2019/2020 asset swap (the innogy transaction), making RWE a related party that exerts significant influence in the IAS 24 sense. E.ON discloses ongoing commercial and operating relationships with RWE Group entities (notably in electricity procurement/sales, network access, and continuing settlement of swap-related arrangements) carried out at arm's length.
- **Associated companies and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method** — including the various energy/network/distribution joint ventures retained from the legacy E.ON portfolio. Transactions comprise primarily energy purchases/sales, network and service charges, and intercompany financing.
- **Pension fund / pension plan assets (Contractual Trust Arrangement)** — E.ON's German pension obligations are funded via a CTA whose assets count as plan assets under IAS 19; the relationship with the funding vehicle is disclosed.
- **Key management personnel (KMP)** — Management Board and Supervisory Board members of E.ON SE. Compensation is disclosed in detail in the Compensation Report; aggregate IAS 24 KMP compensation (short-term, post-employment, share-based) is summarised in this note.

[paraphrased] The note states that transactions with related parties are conducted at arm's length and on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions. It refers to the related-party disclosures of the Management Report (Declaration of Conformity / Dependent Company Report context) where relevant. Transactions among consolidated E.ON Group companies are eliminated on consolidation and are not part of this disclosure.
