## Arm's length statement
[paraphrased — recurring formulation in UNIQA's IAS 24 note] "All transactions are handled under customary market conditions." For the intragroup EUR 215 m loan from UNIQA Re AG to UNIQA Insurance Group AG, UNIQA explicitly states that "the interest rate [is] derived on the basis of the characteristics of the loan and the debtor as well as a comparable bond of UNIQA Insurance Group AG on the capital market" — i.e. priced at arm's length.
