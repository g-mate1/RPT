Siemens' Note 27 — Related party transactions — discloses relationships and transactions with: (a) joint ventures and associates (equity-method investees), most notably the investment in Siemens Energy AG (accounted for as an associate after the spin-off; Siemens holds an indirect economic interest via the Siemens Pension-Trust e.V. and a direct stake reduced over time, approximately 17% on the balance-sheet date), various joint ventures and associates in mobility, infrastructure and digital industries (e.g. Valeo Siemens eAutomotive successor entities and other industrial JVs), as well as several smaller associates; (b) the post-employment benefit plans for the benefit of employees of Siemens (Siemens Pension-Trust e.V., BSAV-Treuhand, and other pension trustees) which hold pension plan assets and to whom Siemens has made contributions during fiscal 2024; (c) the Siemens Stiftung (Siemens Foundation), a non-profit foundation, transactions with which are not material; (d) other related parties under common influence (including von Siemens-Vermögensverwaltung GmbH (vSV), the family office representing the founding Siemens family shareholders, who hold a significant minority shareholding) — transactions are limited to ordinary dividend distributions on shares held; and (e) members of key management personnel (the Managing Board and Supervisory Board of Siemens AG).

The note states that all transactions with related parties are conducted on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions ("at arm's length"). Where Siemens Healthineers AG is concerned, Siemens AG retains majority control (~75%) and Healthineers is fully consolidated; therefore inter-segment transactions are eliminated on consolidation and minority (non-controlling) interest disclosures appear under Note 23 rather than Note 27, while transactions with Siemens Healthineers' non-controlling shareholders (e.g. dividend distributions to NCI) are disclosed separately.

Quantitative disclosure: Siemens presents amounts of sales of goods and services to, and purchases of goods and services from, associated companies and joint ventures, together with year-end receivables and payables (trade and financial) outstanding. In addition, Siemens discloses loans/financing extended to joint ventures and associates (including financing of equity-method investees) and guarantees issued on behalf of such entities. Total compensation of key management personnel (Managing Board) is shown by component (short-term benefits, post-employment benefits, other long-term benefits, share-based payment), with cross-reference to the separate Compensation Report. Supervisory Board fixed and variable remuneration is also disclosed.

## Categories disclosed
- Joint ventures and associates: sales, purchases, receivables, payables, loans (including Siemens Energy AG as an associate)
- Pension/post-employment benefit plans: contributions and balances with Siemens Pension-Trust e.V. and related trustees
- Siemens Stiftung (Foundation): immaterial
- Founding family entities (von Siemens-Vermögensverwaltung GmbH): ordinary-course shareholder relations / dividends only
- Key management personnel (Managing Board and Supervisory Board) compensation — short-term, post-employment, share-based and other long-term
- Guarantees provided to JVs/associates
- Arm's-length statement included
