[paraphrased] Related parties of Erste Group Bank AG comprise:
- **The parent's controlling-influence shareholder group:** DIE ERSTE österreichische Spar-Casse Privatstiftung ("ERSTE Foundation") together with the syndicated savings-bank foundations and the Erste Employees Private Foundation, which act in concert under a shareholders' syndicate agreement.
- **Other shareholders represented in the syndicate:** Caixabank S.A., Wiener Städtische Wechselseitiger Versicherungsverein and the Austrian savings banks party to the Haftungsverbund (cross-guarantee scheme).
- **Subsidiaries not fully consolidated, associates and joint ventures** (including, among others, s IT Solutions group entities accounted for at equity where applicable, real-estate joint ventures, and Erste Social Finance Holding which is a joint venture with ERSTE Foundation).
- **Key management personnel (KMP):** members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board of Erste Group Bank AG and their close family members, plus controlled entities of those persons.

[paraphrased] Banking transactions with these parties (loans, deposits, money-market placements, securities holdings, derivatives and off-balance-sheet commitments) are entered into in the ordinary course of business and **on substantially the same terms — including interest rates and collateral requirements — as comparable transactions with unrelated parties; they do not involve more than the normal risk of collectability or present other unfavourable features**.

[paraphrased] Outstanding balances and the related interest income / expense vis-à-vis ERSTE Foundation (and the broader savings-bank foundations group), associates and joint ventures, and KMP are disclosed in tabular form in the note. The note also separately discloses Management Board and Supervisory Board compensation by IAS 24 category (short-term employee benefits, post-employment benefits, other long-term benefits, termination benefits and share-based payments). Pension obligations to former Management Board members and surviving dependants are likewise reported.
