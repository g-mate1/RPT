Issuer-confirmed recurring sentences include:

> "In the ordinary course of business, Mercedes-Benz AG, its subsidiaries and joint operations conduct business with numerous companies, including related parties. Related parties that are controlled by the Mercedes-Benz Group or over which Mercedes-Benz Group can exercise significant influence are categorized as affiliated, joint ventures (proportionately consolidated joint operations and joint ventures accounted for using the equity method) and associated companies. Business transactions with these parties are generally carried out at market terms." [verbatim — Mercedes-Benz Group, Note 36 Related party relationships, recurring text, FY2024]

> "Related parties of the Mercedes-Benz Group are also natural persons who hold key positions whose changes have to be reported to the Mercedes-Benz Group as well as their close family members. In this context, the Mercedes-Benz Group has business relationships with certain entities related to members of the Board of Management or the Supervisory Board of Mercedes-Benz Group AG. All transactions with these related parties are conducted on an arm's-length basis." [verbatim — Mercedes-Benz Group, Note 36 Related party relationships, recurring text, FY2024]

> "Most of the goods and services supplied within the ordinary course of business between the Mercedes-Benz Group and related parties comprise transactions with joint ventures accounted for using the equity method, particularly Beijing Benz Automotive Co., Ltd. (BBAC), and with associated companies." [verbatim — Mercedes-Benz Group, Note 36, recurring text, FY2024]

[paraphrased] The note discloses that the Chinese partner BAIC (Beijing Automotive Group Co., Ltd. / Beijing Automotive Investment Co., Ltd.) holds approximately 9.98% of Mercedes-Benz Group AG's voting rights and Tenaciou3 Prospect Investment Limited (an investment vehicle controlled by Li Shufu / Geely) holds approximately 9.69% — neither holding constitutes control under IFRS 10, but transactions with the BAIC group and entities related to Li Shufu are nevertheless disclosed because Mercedes-Benz Group has business relationships with their subsidiaries and joint ventures.

[paraphrased] The principal categories of related party transactions reported in Note 36 for FY2024 are: (i) joint ventures accounted for using the equity method — principally Beijing Benz Automotive Co., Ltd. (BBAC; 49% MB-AG/51% BAIC); smart Automobile Co., Ltd. (50/50 JV with Geely Group); Fujian Benz Automotive Co., Ltd. (FBAC); Mercedes-Benz Trucks Vostok (deconsolidated 2022 — residual disclosure); Mercedes-Benz Auto Finance Ltd. (MBAFC); various financial-services JVs; (ii) associated companies including There Holding B.V. / FREENOW, ChargePoint Holdings Inc. (residual), Stardust Holdings, YASA Limited (now subsidiary), Rivian Automotive (former); (iii) unconsolidated subsidiaries; and (iv) entities related to BAIC and to Li Shufu/Geely (cross-shareholdings and joint operations).

[paraphrased] The note also discloses guarantees / sureties provided by Mercedes-Benz Group to joint ventures and associated companies (predominantly to BBAC and to financial-services JVs), royalty / technology license income from BBAC (the technology licensing agreement generated approximately EUR 189 million in 2024) and dividends received from equity-accounted investees (BBAC remained the principal contributor in 2024 although dividends declined materially versus the BBAC payout reported for FY2025 of approximately EUR 881 million).

[paraphrased] Note 36 includes the standard summary table "Transactions with related parties" presenting, for joint ventures, associated companies, and other related parties separately: (a) sales of goods and services, (b) purchases of goods and services, (c) receivables outstanding at year-end, (d) payables outstanding at year-end, (e) guarantees / contingent liabilities. The aggregate transactions are dominated by BBAC sales (vehicle kits / CKD components sold to BBAC for local assembly in Beijing) and corresponding receivables at year-end.

[paraphrased] Key management personnel (KMP) compensation — total compensation of the Board of Management and Supervisory Board members of Mercedes-Benz Group AG — is disclosed at the end of Note 36 with cross-reference to the audited 2024 Remuneration Report (per § 162 AktG), which forms part of the Annual Report. Total Board of Management remuneration for FY2024 declined versus FY2023 (the FY2023 figure was elevated by long-term incentive payouts, with CEO Ola Källenius reporting approximately EUR 12.1 million for FY2023).

## Categories disclosed
- Joint ventures accounted for using the equity method (principally BBAC — Beijing Benz Automotive Co., Ltd. 49/51; smart Automobile Co., Ltd. 50/50 with Geely; Fujian Benz Automotive Co., Ltd.; financial-services JVs).
- Associated companies (equity-method investees including There Holding B.V. / FREENOW residual, and other minority equity stakes).
- Unconsolidated subsidiaries.
- Major shareholders deemed related parties because of their joint-venture / cross-shareholding relationships: BAIC Group (approx. 9.98% of voting rights) and Tenaciou3 Prospect Investment Limited / Li Shufu — Geely (approx. 9.69% of voting rights).
- Sales of goods and services to joint ventures and associated companies (predominantly CKD / vehicle kit sales to BBAC; powertrain and component sales to JVs).
- Purchases of goods and services from joint ventures and associated companies.
- Receivables from joint ventures, associated companies and other related parties (trade and financial).
- Payables to joint ventures, associated companies and other related parties (trade and financial).
- Guarantees / sureties / contingent liabilities provided to JVs and associates (predominantly BBAC and financial-services JVs).
- Royalty / technology license income from BBAC (approx. EUR 189 million in 2024).
- Dividends received from equity-accounted investees (BBAC the principal contributor).
- Key management personnel compensation — Board of Management and Supervisory Board (cross-referenced to the audited Remuneration Report 2024).
- Pension and post-employment benefits to former Board members and their survivors (within KMP disclosure).
- Transactions with entities related to members of the Board of Management or Supervisory Board (entities in which Board members hold key positions or have close family relationships) — disclosed as immaterial and at arm's length.
