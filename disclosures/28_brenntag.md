[paraphrased] Related parties of the Brenntag Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise:
- the members of the Board of Management of Brenntag SE,
- the members of the Supervisory Board of Brenntag SE, and
- the non-consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates of the Brenntag Group.

[paraphrased] Brenntag SE has no controlling shareholder; the share capital is held by a broad free float. There were therefore no transactions to be reported with a parent company or other controlling investor during the financial year.

[paraphrased] Business relationships with non-consolidated subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures arise in the ordinary course of business (purchases and sales of chemical products, distribution services, allocations of administrative costs) and are concluded on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions. The volumes are not material to the consolidated financial statements.

[paraphrased] Key management personnel within the meaning of IAS 24 are the members of the Board of Management and of the Supervisory Board of Brenntag SE. Their compensation comprises short-term employee benefits (fixed salary, fringe benefits and short-term variable remuneration), post-employment benefits (service cost for pension entitlements), other long-term benefits and share-based payments under the long-term incentive plan (Performance Share Plan).

[paraphrased] Total compensation of the Board of Management for FY2024 amounted to a low single-digit million euro figure (KPI-disclosed in the Remuneration Report pursuant to Sec. 162 AktG; CEO Dr Christian Kohlpaintner, CFO Dr Kristin Neumann, divisional CEOs Michael Friede and Ewout van Jarwaarde). Total compensation of the Supervisory Board (chaired by Doreen Nowotne) was disclosed at low seven-digit euro level. Further details on individual remuneration are given in the separately published Remuneration Report, which forms an integral part of the 2024 Annual Report and is audited pursuant to Sec. 162 (3) AktG.

[paraphrased] No loans or advances were granted to members of the Board of Management or the Supervisory Board, and no guarantees were assumed in their favour during the reporting period. Apart from a consulting agreement entered into in the ordinary course with a former member of the Board of Management (Supervisory Board approved), there were no other reportable transactions with related natural persons.
