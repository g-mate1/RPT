Related parties of BAWAG Group AG comprise:
- The ultimate parent and its subsidiaries that are not fully consolidated;
- Subsidiaries that are not consolidated, associates and joint ventures;
- Members of the Management Board and the Supervisory Board of BAWAG Group AG (key management personnel — KMP) and their close family members;
- Other entities in which KMP or their close family members exercise control, joint control or significant influence.

Transactions with related parties (including loans, current accounts, deposits, guarantees and other ordinary banking transactions) are carried out in the normal course of business and on the same terms as comparable transactions with unrelated third parties (i.e. on an arm's-length basis). Transactions with members of the Management Board, the Supervisory Board and their close relatives are subject to individual prior approval by the Risk and Credit Committee, with explicit statement of the terms and conditions; blanket pre-approvals are not permitted for this group of persons. Intra-group transactions are structured in line with applicable transfer-pricing regulations on the arm's-length principle.
