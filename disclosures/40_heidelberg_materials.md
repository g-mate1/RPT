## Verbatim disclosure
[paraphrased — full text retrieval blocked by HTTP 403 on issuer PDFs; the substance below is reconstructed from search snippets of the same PDFs plus the AG separate financial statements 2024 and prior-year disclosures of identical structure]

"Related parties of the Heidelberg Materials Group within the meaning of IAS 24 comprise the members of the Managing Board and the Supervisory Board of Heidelberg Materials AG (key management personnel), their close family members, as well as subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates of the Heidelberg Materials Group. In addition, Spohn Cement Beteiligungen GmbH, Schönefeld/Germany, which is controlled by Mr Ludwig Merckle, is a related party as more than 25% of the voting rights in Heidelberg Materials AG are attributed to Mr Merckle via this entity (28.88% of the issued share capital as of 31 December 2024). Mr Ludwig Merckle is a member of the Supervisory Board of Heidelberg Materials AG."

"Transactions with subsidiaries that are fully consolidated in the consolidated financial statements are eliminated on consolidation and not disclosed as related-party transactions. Transactions with non-consolidated subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates are carried out at arm's length conditions, in the ordinary course of business, and mainly comprise sales of goods, purchases of raw materials and clinker, the provision and procurement of services, as well as financing arrangements (including cash-pooling and intercompany loans) and the granting of guarantees." [paraphrased]

Per the separate AG financial statements 2024 (HM AG, parent company, HGB) — disclosed indicatively to illustrate the magnitude of intra-group RPTs:

- Liabilities to subsidiaries: approx. EUR 13.1 bn (intra-group financing/cash pooling)
- Transactions with subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates (HM AG, FY2024): Disposals EUR 41.6 m / 28.1 m / 8.3 m; Acquisitions EUR 13.1 m / 0.0 m / 13.6 m; Procured services EUR 24.2 m / 0.1 m / 0.1 m; Provided services EUR 44.4 m / 5.9 m / 9.0 m (subsidiaries / joint ventures / associates respectively)

"No material transactions between Heidelberg Materials Group companies and Spohn Cement Beteiligungen GmbH or other entities controlled by Mr Ludwig Merckle, or with close family members of members of the Managing Board or the Supervisory Board, took place in the financial year 2024." Compensation of key management personnel (Managing Board and Supervisory Board) is disclosed in detail in the Remuneration Report 2024 (separate report; https://www.heidelbergmaterials.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/HM_Remuneration_report_2024.pdf).
