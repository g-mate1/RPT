## Categories disclosed
- Transactions with non-consolidated subsidiaries (supply of raw materials / sale of finished goods; arm's length).
- Transactions with associates (equity-method investees, including ArcelorMittal Texas HBI Group — 20% stake; supply of HBI).
- Transactions with joint ventures (supply relationships; arm's length).
- Receivables from and liabilities to non-consolidated subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures (direct and indirect).
- Factoring agreements with core shareholders (receivables sold to core shareholders at arm's length; related interest expense disclosed).
- Reverse-factoring / supplier-finance arrangements with core shareholders (FY2023/24 disclosure: nil at the balance-sheet date; FY2024/25 reaffirmed; covered cross-reference in note D.22 Supplier finance arrangements).
- Key management personnel (KMP) compensation — Management Board and Supervisory Board (full details in the audited Compensation Report 2024/25 cross-referenced from D.26).
- Core shareholders (Raiffeisenlandesbank Oberösterreich Invest GmbH & Co OG 15.0%; voestalpine Mitarbeiterbeteiligung Privatstiftung employee foundation).
- No material impact statement on non-inclusion of non-consolidated entities.

## Quantitative highlights (EUR m)
- Sales to RP / revenue from associates, JVs and non-consolidated subsidiaries: disclosed in the note but exact FY2024/25 figure not retrievable via search (declined materially in 2023/24 following expiry of ArcelorMittal Texas HBI raw-material deliveries; FY2024/25 reflects only the residual HBI off-take and routine intra-group supply).
- Purchases from RP: disclosed (purchases of HBI from ArcelorMittal Texas HBI Group and raw-materials/finished-goods purchases from other associates and non-consolidated subsidiaries); precise FY2024/25 number not retrievable via search.
- Receivables from RP (associates, JVs, non-consolidated subsidiaries): disclosed; precise FY2024/25 number not retrievable via search.
- Payables to RP (associates, JVs, non-consolidated subsidiaries): disclosed; precise FY2024/25 number not retrievable via search.
- Factoring with core shareholders (receivables sold): FY2023/24 EUR 191.5 m (FY2022/23: EUR 230.7 m); FY2024/25 figure stated in the note but not retrievable via search.
- Interest expense on factoring with core shareholders: FY2023/24 EUR 5.4 m (FY2022/23: EUR 2.8 m); FY2024/25 figure stated in the note but not retrievable via search.
- Reverse-factoring payables to core shareholders at 31 March 2025: nil (no trade payables from bills of exchange and reverse-factoring arrangements with core shareholders at the reporting date) [paraphrased — recurring statement].
- KMP compensation FY2024/25 (per audited Compensation Report cross-referenced from D.26):
