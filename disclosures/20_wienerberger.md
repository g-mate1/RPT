## Verbatim disclosure
"Related parties include the Managing Board and their close family members, associated companies, joint ventures, non-consolidated subsidiaries of Wienerberger AG as well as ANC Privatstiftung and its subsidiaries. [...] ANC Privatstiftung and its participations operate the landfill business in Austria that was transferred to it by Wienerberger AG in 2001 and own a limited amount of assets (in particular real estate and securities). The managing board of ANC Privatstiftung consists of three members, one of them a member of wienerberger's top management.

Wienerberger AG and its subsidiaries grant loans to associates, joint ventures and non-consolidated subsidiaries on arm's length conditions. As at 31/12/2024, the outstanding non-interest-bearing loans to joint ventures amounted to TEUR 21,285 (2023: TEUR 22,162), while interest-bearing loans to non-consolidated affiliated companies amounted to TEUR 3,908 (2023: TEUR 4,071).

Revenues of TEUR 922 (2023: TEUR 853) were generated with joint ventures in the reporting year. Further transactions with related parties concerned clay deliveries in the amount of TEUR 390 (2023: TEUR 565) and rent payments in the amount of TEUR 457 (2023: TEUR 404). Moreover, products worth TEUR 1,340 (2023: 991) were sold to a company with related-party status. Trade receivables due from joint ventures in the amount of TEUR 53 (2023: TEUR 102), including the sale of plant and equipment, were recognized as of the balance sheet date.

Transactions with natural persons with related-party status made in 2024 amounted to TEUR 149 (2023: TEUR 258). [...]

Transactions with companies in which members of the Supervisory Board of Wienerberger AG are active are conducted at arm's length. [...]

In the reporting year, the fixed remuneration component of the Managing Board members amounted to TEUR 2,660 (2023: TEUR 2,540). [...] Remuneration of the members of the Supervisory Board for the 2024 business year came to a total of EUR 1,068,750 (2023: 889,795)."

[Paraphrased – Remuneration Report 2024] One Managing Board member's service contract was terminated by mutual agreement effective 31/12/2024 and the board member received statutory severance pay of EUR 463 thousand (paid January 2025). Provision for severance entitlements relating to Managing Board members amounted to EUR 1,235,704 in 2024. Managing Board members are covered by an external employee severance fund (Betriebliche Mitarbeitervorsorgekasse). Change-of-control clauses (>20% shareholding threshold) and post-contractual non-competition clauses are part of the Managing Board contracts.
