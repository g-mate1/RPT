## Verbatim disclosure
[paraphrased from the published HTML version of Note 39, as the secured PDF could not be retrieved directly]

"In accordance with IAS 24 'Related Party Disclosures', the Supervisory Board and the Executive Board of adidas AG have been identified as related parties who receive compensation essentially in connection with their function as key management personnel of adidas AG. Detailed information about the compensation of the Supervisory Board and the Executive Board of adidas AG is presented in Note 40 (Other Information) and in the Compensation Report.

A brand ambassador agreement was in place between adidas and Supervisory Board member Jackie Joyner-Kersee, under which adidas paid fixed compensation of € 0.2 million in 2024.

adidas Pension Trust e.V., a registered association based in Herzogenaurach, is regarded as a related party of adidas AG. Based on a Contractual Trust Arrangement (CTA), adidas Pension Trust e.V. manages the plan assets in the form of an administrative trust to fund and protect part of the pension obligations of adidas AG. adidas AG has the right to claim a refund of pension payments from adidas Pension Trust e.V. under specific contractually agreed conditions. As of December 31, 2024, adidas Pension Trust e.V. held plan assets of € 392.5 million in trust for adidas AG. In 2024, adidas AG made lease payments of € 7.0 million to adidas Pension Trust e.V. As of December 31, 2024, outstanding liabilities to adidas Pension Trust e.V. amounted to € 0.6 million.

The non-profit foundation adidas Stiftung, Herzogenaurach, established in 2023, together with its subsidiary, is considered a related party of adidas AG. In 2023, adidas AG entered into a donation agreement with the foundation, committing to make a donation in a total amount of € 115.3 million over several years to the foundation. In 2024, adidas AG transferred € 16.9 million to the foundation based on the foundation's drawdown in accordance with the donation agreement. In October 2024, adidas AG entered into a further donation agreement with the foundation, committing to make another donation in a total amount of € 106.0 million over 10 years. This amount was outstanding in full as of December 31, 2024 and recognized as other liability.

[...] Transactions with related parties are conducted on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's length transactions."

## Categories disclosed
- Key management personnel (KMP) compensation — Executive Board and Supervisory Board (cross-reference to Note 40 / Compensation Report)
- Brand ambassador service contract with a Supervisory Board member (Jackie Joyner-Kersee)
- Pension trust (adidas Pension Trust e.V.) — plan assets, lease payments, outstanding liabilities
- Non-profit foundation (adidas Stiftung) — multi-year donation commitments, drawdowns, outstanding liability
- Implicit lease arrangement (real estate) with adidas Pension Trust e.V.

## Quantitative highlights (EUR m)
- Sales to RP: not disclosed (n/a — no associates/JVs of material size)
- Purchases from RP: € 0.2 m (brand-ambassador fee to Supervisory Board member J. Joyner-Kersee)
- Receivables from RP: not disclosed
- Payables to RP: € 0.6 m (to adidas Pension Trust e.V.) + € 106.0 m other liability (donation commitment to adidas Stiftung) = ~€ 106.6 m
- Loans to/from RP: none disclosed
- KMP compensation: cross-referenced to Note 40 / Compensation Report (Executive Board & Supervisory Board)
- Lease payments to RP: € 7.0 m (to adidas Pension Trust e.V.)
- Donations (charity/foundation): € 16.9 m transferred in 2024; commitments € 115.3 m (2023 agreement) + € 106.0 m (Oct 2024 agreement)
- Plan assets held by Pension Trust: € 392.5 m (off-balance-sheet trust asset)

Aggregate identifiable RPT volume in 2024 ≈ € 24.1 m (P&L impact: € 7.0 m lease + € 16.9 m donation transfer + € 0.2 m ambassador fee), plus € 106.0 m new long-term commitment recognized as liability, plus KMP compensation per Note 40/Compensation Report.
