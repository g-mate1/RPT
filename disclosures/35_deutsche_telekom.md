"The Federal Republic of Germany is both a direct and an indirect shareholder (via KfW Bankengruppe) and holds 27.8 % (December 31, 2023: 30.5 %) of the share capital of Deutsche Telekom AG."

"Thanks to higher levels of attendance, the Federal Republic has not had a majority of the voting rights at the Shareholders' Meetings of Deutsche Telekom AG since 2016. As such, it is no longer deemed to have control over Deutsche Telekom, but rather only a significant influence. As a result, the Federal Republic and the companies controlled and jointly controlled by the Federal Republic, but not the companies over which the Federal Republic can exercise a significant influence, are classified as related parties of Deutsche Telekom."

"The Federal Republic and the companies controlled and jointly controlled by the Federal Republic are customers or suppliers of Deutsche Telekom and as such have mutual contractual relationships with Deutsche Telekom. In the course of business, Deutsche Telekom deals directly with these companies, and with authorities and other government agencies as an independent party." [paraphrased]

## Categories disclosed
- Government-related entity disclosure under IAS 24.25–27: the Federal Republic of Germany (direct + KfW = 27.8 %) and entities it controls / jointly controls
- Joint ventures accounted for using the equity method: GD tower companies (Germany & Austria), GlasfaserPlus, Glasfaser NordWest
- Associates: DIV II (no material balances)
- Key management personnel: Board of Management and Supervisory Board (short-term, long-term/share-based, post-employment/pension obligations)
- Shareholder loans granted to joint ventures
- Sale-and-leaseback arrangement with GD tower companies (lease liabilities of EUR 4.6 bn)
- Revenues / receivables / payables with joint ventures
