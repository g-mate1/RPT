The following is a paraphrased summary of Henkel's standard "Related parties" disclosure pattern as carried into FY2024, consistent with publicly available extracts of the 2024 Annual Report and remuneration report.]

[paraphrased] In accordance with IAS 24, Henkel discloses relationships and transactions with related companies and persons. Henkel AG & Co. KGaA is a German partnership limited by shares (Kommanditgesellschaft auf Aktien, KGaA). Its sole Personally Liable Partner (Komplementär) is Henkel Management AG, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Henkel AG & Co. KGaA whose Supervisory Board is chaired by Dr. Simone Bagel-Trah. Henkel Management AG receives reimbursement of its expenses incurred in managing the business of Henkel AG & Co. KGaA, including the remuneration of its Management Board and its Supervisory Board, plus a statutory remuneration based on its share capital.

[paraphrased] A significant portion of the ordinary shares is held by members of the Henkel family who are parties to the Henkel share-binding agreement (Henkel-Bindungsvertrag / "Aktienbindungsvertrag"), forming a voting pool of approximately 61.79% of the voting rights. Members of this pool, who are also represented on the Shareholders' Committee and Supervisory Board, are therefore considered related parties.

[paraphrased] Related-party transactions in the period consisted of:
- the Personally Liable Partner relationship with Henkel Management AG (reimbursement of expenses and statutory partner remuneration);
- transactions in the ordinary course of business with non-consolidated subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures, immaterial in aggregate;
- compensation paid to key management personnel (members of the Management Board of Henkel Management AG, the Supervisory Board and the Shareholders' Committee of Henkel AG & Co. KGaA), as detailed in the Remuneration Report;
- ordinary dividend distributions to family shareholders on the same terms as to all other shareholders.

[paraphrased] All transactions with related parties were conducted on an arm's-length basis on terms equivalent to those that prevail in arm's-length transactions.

[paraphrased] Key management personnel compensation (Management Board, Supervisory Board, Shareholders' Committee — current and former members combined) is disclosed in summary form. Based on the 2024 Remuneration Report, total target Management Board remuneration at 100% target achievement was approximately EUR 14–15 million; total Supervisory Board fees and Shareholders' Committee fees added several million EUR more. Pension and similar long-term benefit obligations for former members of the Management Board are recognised as part of the Group's pension provisions.
