Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA ("Fresenius SE") is the Company's largest shareholder and owns 94,380,382 of the Company's shares as of February 13, 2025, representing 32.2% of the 293,413,449 outstanding shares. The Else Kröner-Fresenius-Stiftung is the sole shareholder of Fresenius Management SE, the general partner of Fresenius SE, and has sole power to elect the supervisory board of Fresenius Management SE. Under the Company's Articles of Association, Fresenius SE has the right to appoint two of the six shareholder representatives to the Company's Supervisory Board.

The Company has entered into certain arrangements for services and products with Fresenius SE or its subsidiaries (the "Fresenius SE Companies") and with certain of the Company's equity method investees, as well as arrangements for leases with Fresenius SE or its subsidiaries. The Company's terms related to the receivables or payables for these services, leases and products are generally consistent with the normal terms of the Company's ordinary course of business transactions with unrelated parties, and the Company believes that these arrangements reflect fair market terms.

Prior to and in connection with the Conversion of Fresenius Medical Care AG & Co. KGaA into Fresenius Medical Care AG (effective November 30, 2023), the Company was party to service agreements with Fresenius SE and certain of its affiliates to receive services, including administrative services, management information services, employee benefit administration, insurance, information technology services, tax services and treasury management services. In connection with and subsequent to the Conversion, the Company entered into transition services agreements with Fresenius SE Companies to receive services, including administrative and facility management services, employee benefit administration, insurance brokerage, information technology, intellectual property and certain treasury services. These transitional arrangements were entered into for periods of several months up to 2 years, with extension options. The Company also entered into various longer-term service agreements with Fresenius SE Companies covering fixed-asset accounting and IT/communications-related services for up to one year.

A Trademark License Agreement with Fresenius SE remained in effect following the Conversion, with amendments regarding use of the "Fresenius Marks" and reporting obligations regarding any harmful use of the licensed marks and the "Fresenius" name.

The Company leases facilities in Germany on a long-term basis from Fresenius SE or one of its affiliates. The Company also purchases pharmaceutical products and other supplies from Fresenius Kabi (a Fresenius SE subsidiary).

The Company also has transactions with its equity method investees (joint ventures and associates), including sales of dialysis products and provision/receipt of services on arm's-length terms.

Compensation of key management personnel (members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board) is disclosed individually in the Compensation Report, which forms part of the Annual Report 2024 ("Building the Momentum Compensation Report 2024"). [...]
